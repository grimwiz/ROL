Period grounding for a 1920s game. The rulebook's timeline runs three parallel tracks by year — real-world **Events**, **strange phenomena** ("the Universe according to Fort, et al."), and **Disasters** — reproduced in full below. The source also includes a complete 1920 calendar (Sun-Sat, all twelve months) as a starting point for real-time campaign dating.

## Period Economy

The dollar is convertible to gold; inflation runs ~1%/year. No sales tax; federal income tax ~5%/year only over $20,000. Banks are independent and uninsured (a rate above ~3% signals a shaky bank).

Average price of one fine ounce on the London market (dollars):

| Year | Gold | Silver |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1920 | 27.49 | 1.35 |
| 1923 | 21.96 | .67 |
| 1926 | 20.66 | .63 |
| 1929 | 20.67 | .54 |
| 1930 | 20.68 | .39 |

Sample yearly wages, US railroad labour, 1920: officer ~$6,300; conductor ~$2,970; foreman ~$2,250; carpenter ~$1,800; clerk ~$1,650; labourer ~$1,300. (By 1928 many had fallen as the industry weakened.)

## Travel and Distances

Transport speeds and costs are in `10-equipment-and-the-1920s.md`. The keys to long travel are the steamship and the railroad; the automobile and aircraft are young and not yet relied on for speed or comfort.

**Largest liners (1920), by registered tonnage:** *Leviathan* (54,282), *Imperator* (52,000), *Europa* (50,000), *Aquitania* (47,000), *Olympic* (45,000), *Mauretania* (32,000).

**Sample land distances (miles), New York / New Orleans / San Francisco:**

| To | from NY | from NO | from SF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Chicago | 912 | 912 | 2,279 |
| Denver | 1,930 | 1,357 | 1,376 |
| Los Angeles | 3,149 | 2,007 | 475 |
| Seattle | 3,151 | 2,931 | 957 |
| Washington, D.C. | 228 | 1,144 | 3,069 |

**Sea distances from the Panama Canal (nautical miles):** New York 1,974; San Francisco 3,245; Hamburg 5,070; Yokohama 7,682; Hongkong 9,195; Sydney 7,674.

**Notable records (by 1930):** fastest trans-Atlantic crossing New York-Cherbourg ~5 days; fastest day's steamship run (*Mauretania*) ~676 nautical miles at ~27 knots; fastest trip around the world 23 days 15 hours.
