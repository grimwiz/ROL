Mythos magic is not the wizardry of fantasy: it is the manipulation of alien forces, learned at the cost of Sanity. This file covers the mechanics an investigator needs. Which tomes, spells, and beings actually exist is for the Keeper to reveal in play.

## The Cthulhu Mythos Skill

- Base 0%; maximum 98% for a player-character.
- Never chosen at creation and never raised by experience or skill points.
- Gained only by insane insight (`07-sanity.md`) or by reading forbidden tomes. Increases are permanent and cannot be forgotten.
- Caps maximum SAN at `99 − Cthulhu Mythos`.
- A successful roll lets an investigator identify a Mythos creature's general class (its exact type if seen clearly), recognise a spell as it is cast, or identify a Mythos tome from a page or two.

## Magic Points

- Magic Points (MP) start equal to POW.
- They regenerate at **¼ POW per 6 hours** (round down) — a full pool over a day. They never exceed POW.
- At **0 MP** the character falls unconscious until MP returns to at least 1.

## Learning Spells

An investigator who acquires a spell does so by:

- **Reading a Mythos tome** — each grants a Cthulhu Mythos bonus and may teach its spells, and costs the reader Sanity.
- **Being taught** — one-on-one; each week, roll INT or less on `D100` to learn.
- **A deity or monster** — via a tablet or scroll, or a telepathic implant (retained on a successful Idea roll).

The specific tomes, their effects, and the spells they hold are revealed by the Keeper.

## Casting Spells

Five requirements must be met:

1. **Magic points** (or, for some spells, permanent **POW** — POW does not regenerate).
2. **Physical component** — reusable for some spells, consumed by others.
3. **Time** — from seconds to over a week.
4. **Incantation** — the caster needs freedom of movement and usually voice.
5. **Sanity** — most spells cost SAN automatically; no SAN roll reduces it.

Broadly, spells **Summon**, **Bind**, **Contact**, **Call**, or **Dismiss** Mythos beings, or produce a direct effect (a curse, a ward, an enchantment). Summoning and offensive spells generally pit the caster's magic points (or POW) against the target on the Resistance Table (`02-core-resolution.md`).

## Increasing POW

When a character succeeds at a spell that matched his MP or POW against a target on the Resistance Table, POW may rise: chance = `(21 − current POW) × 5%` to gain `1D3` POW.

Other routes: sacrifice 10 SAN for 1 POW (not possible at 9 or less SAN), or bargain POW from a deity (the Keeper's call).
