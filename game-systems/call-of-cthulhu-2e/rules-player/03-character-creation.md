Create an investigator with the steps below. Characteristic definitions, derived attributes, and the Damage Bonus Table are in `01-characteristics.md`; skills are in `04-skills.md`.

## Procedure

1. **Name and nationality.** Default investigators are American; non-Americans are usually European. All are assumed to speak English well.
2. **Roll characteristics.** STR, CON, POW, DEX, APP on `3D6`; SIZ and INT on `2D6+6`; EDU on `3D6+3`.
3. **Derive attributes.** Idea (INT×5), Luck (POW×5), Know (EDU×5), Magic Points (= POW), Hit Points ((SIZ+CON)÷2), Damage Bonus (from STR+SIZ), Move 8.
4. **Set Sanity.** Starting SAN = POW×5. Record maximum SAN as `99` (no Cthulhu Mythos yet); maximum SAN is always `99 − Cthulhu Mythos %`.
5. **Choose an occupation** and spend occupation skill points (below).
6. **Spend personal skill points.**
7. **Record combat skills** under Weapons on the sheet (see `05-combat.md`).

## Occupations

Choose one occupation:

- Journalist (newspaper, magazine)
- Parapsychologist
- Professor
- Historian/Antiquarian
- Author (novelist, freelance, etc.)
- Private Investigator
- Dilettante
- Other (Doctor, Lawyer, Clergyman, etc., as determined by the Keeper)

## Skill Points

| Pool | Points | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| Occupation | EDU × 15 | Only the skills listed for the chosen occupation. |
| Personal | INT × 5 | Any skills, including combat skills and skills outside the occupation. |

- Each point raises a skill by 1 percentage point, added to its base chance (see `04-skills.md`).
- Points not allocated are lost.
- A native speaker's Read/Write (own language) already stands at EDU×5 before points are spent.
- The Cthulhu Mythos skill cannot be raised with skill points (see `07-sanity.md` and `08-magic-and-mythos.md`).

## Money and Equipment

Starting wealth, income, and savings derive from the period economy; equipment and prices are in `10-equipment-and-the-1920s.md`. Credit Rating represents an investigator's financial standing.
