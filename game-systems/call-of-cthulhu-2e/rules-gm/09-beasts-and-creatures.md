Generic natural and unnatural creatures for ordinary 1920s play — distinct from the Cthulhu Mythos beings (Keeper-only). The Keeper may resize an animal by adding `1D6` or two to STR/CON/SIZ (and raising its damage to match). Stat blocks give the average characteristics; Attk lists Weapon — % (damage).

## Potential Sanity Losses

| Creature | SAN loss |
|---|---|
| Bat, Bear, Dog, Condor, Crocodile, Gorilla, Horse, Lion, Elephant, Python, Rat, Squid, Tiger, Wasp Swarm, Wolf | None |
| Ghost, Wraith, Zombie | 1D8+ |
| Mummy, Werewolf | 1D8 |
| Human Skeleton, Vampire | 1D6 |
| Pixie | 1D3 |

## Animals

### Bat, Large
`STR 5 · CON 7 · SIZ 5 · POW 7 · DEX 21-22` — **HP 6, Move 1 / 12 fly**. Bite 40% (1D4). Spot Hidden 75%.

### Bear, Common Black
`STR 16-17 · CON 13 · SIZ 16-17 · POW 10-11 · DEX 10-11` — **HP 15, Move 8**. Bite 45% (1D6+1D8), Claw 30% (1D6+1D6), Hug 30% (3D6). Per round: bite + claw twice (up to three foes), **or** bite + hug (one foe). Armor 2. Track 40%, Climb 50%.

### Dog, Ordinary
`STR 7 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 4-5 · POW 7 · DEX 13` — **HP 8, Move 12**. Bite 30% (1D6). Track by Smell 80%, Spot Hidden 60%. Packs of `1D8+3`.

### Condor or Eagle, Giant
`STR 22-23 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 16-17 · POW 13 · DEX 19` — **HP 14, Move 5 / 12 fly**. Bite 45% (1D8+1D6), Claw 45% (1D6+1D6). Armor 2. A flying bird halves an attacker's chance to hit it.

### Crocodile, Nine Yards Long
`STR 40-41 · CON 19 · SIZ 40-41 · POW 10-11 · DEX 10` — **HP 30, Move 7**. Bite 60% (1D10+4D6), Tail Lash 60% (1D6+4D6) — may strike simultaneously, different foes. In water it clings with the bite and spins (damage bonus/round); a tail-lash victim rolls STR×5 or falls. Armor 10. (Garden-variety croc: ÷2-3 on STR/CON/SIZ/POW, damage +1D6.)

### Gorilla
`STR 26 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 19 · INT 4-5 · POW 10-11 · DEX 16-17` — **HP 15, Move 8**. Bite 45% (1D6+2D6), Hand 45% (1D6) — both hands hit → grapple (damage bonus/round until STR vs STR break). Armor 2. Normally shy and peaceful.

### Horses
Three classes. The **cart horse** (also mule, burro, llama) flees from anger or gunfire. The **cavalry horse** stays calm in battle but does not fight independently (a rider whose horse fights must spend his turn on Ride rolls). The **riding horse** will not fight unless cornered.
Riding horse: `STR 28-29 · CON 13 · SIZ 26 · POW 10-11 · DEX 10-11` — **HP 20, Move 12**. Bite 05% (1D10), Kick 05% (1D8+2D6), Rear/Plunge 05% (2D8+2D6), Trample 25% (4D6 to a downed foe). Armor 1.

### Lion
`STR 19 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 16-17 · POW 13 · DEX 19` — **HP 14, Move 10**. Bite 40% (1D10+1D6), Claw 60% (1D6+1D6), Ripping 80% (4D6). Two claws + one bite, same foe; both claws hit → hang on, rip with hind legs and keep biting. Armor 2.

### Tiger
`STR 22-23 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 16-17 · POW 10-11 · DEX 19` — **HP 13, Move 10**. Bite 45% (1D10+1D6), Claw 70% (1D8+1D6), Ripping 80% (2D8+2D6). As Lion (two claws + bite, same foe; both claws → rip). Armor 2. Move Quietly 75%, Hide 80%, Track 50%.

### Elephant (or Mammoth)
`STR 50 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 50 · POW 10-11 · DEX 7` — **HP 31, Move 10**. Gore 65% (1D8+5D6), Trample 75% (10D6). Instead of goring it may seize with its trunk (same %): the victim rolls STR vs half the beast's STR or is set up to be trampled next round, or is flung (falling damage by ~1 yard per 3 STR). The sensitive trunk is used to fight only when the beast has lost half its HP.

### Python (very large snake)
`STR 22-23 · CON 13 · SIZ 17-18 · POW 10-11 · DEX 13` — **HP 16, Move 6**. Bite 40% (1D6), Crush 60% (2D6). Entrancing gaze: POW vs POW; an entranced victim cannot defend, and is hit at +20%. A successful crush enfolds the victim (2D6/round, armor helps; CON×5 or fall unconscious; free the weapon arm on DEX×3). Armor 2. Move Quietly 90%, Hide 75%.

### Squid, Giant (~4 yards)
`STR 21 · CON 13 · SIZ 21 · INT 7 · POW 10-11 · DEX 19` — **HP 17, Move 4 / 10 swim**. Beak 45% (1D10 + poison, potency = CON), Tentacle 45% (1D6) — beak and eight tentacles strike at once, up to eight targets; two tentacles on one foe constrict for 4D6/round (armor helps). Armor 2. Hide 70%.

### Wolf
`STR 13 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 10-11 · POW 10-11 · DEX 13` — **HP 10-11, Move 12**. Bite 30% (1D8). Armor 1. Track by Smell 80%, Spot Hidden 60%. May hunt in packs; normally shy.

### Rat Packs
Rats attack as packs. Set the number of packs (e.g. `1D8+2`); the pack attack chance is `packs × 5%`, doing `1D3` on a hit (armor absorbs each). Investigators always strike first; a PC who kills a rat scatters one pack. (For description, multiply packs by ~10 for the number of rats.)

### Wasp Swarm (or enraged bees)
Stings 15% for `1D10` stings (1 damage each), lasting `2D6` rounds; no protection unless fully covered or enclosed.

## Undead and Legendary

> Ghosts and wraiths should be hand-crafted by the Keeper for each haunting.

### Ghost
Has only INT and POW, appearing as mist. Each round it matches POW vs the victim's POW; if it wins, the victim loses `1D3` POW; if the victim wins, the ghost loses `1D3` POW. Powerful ghosts may drain `1D6`+ POW but still lose only `1D3` when overcome. SAN 1D8+.

### Wraith
A hate-born ghost (POW rarely below 13), malignant to the living, vulnerable to magic (its POW acts as CON vs magical attack). It matches POW vs the victim's CON; on a win the victim loses points of STR, INT, or CON equal to the wraith's POW (once per victim) — a characteristic below 1 is death. Thereafter it attacks the victim's POW (lose `1D6−1`/round). At 0 HP it dissolves and cannot reform for at least a week. SAN 1D8+.

### Zombie
`STR & CON ×1.5` (15-17) · `SIZ 10-11 · POW 1 · DEX 7` — **HP 13-14, Move 6**. Maul 35% (2D8+1D6). Almost immune to impaling weapons (firearms included): each impale does 1 CON damage; other weapons do half rolled damage, and it must be hacked apart. Hits at DEX×5, parries at half. POW (1) comes from the animating spell; the caster directs it. SAN 1D8+.

### Human Skeleton
`STR 10-11 · SIZ 10-11 · POW 10-11 · DEX 13`, CON N/A — **HP 9, Move 8**. Brittle: a hit destroys it on `(damage × 4)%` (impaling weapons `× 2%`); otherwise it is undamaged. Weapon by `1D6`: 1 sword (DEX×4%, 1D8+1), 2 axe (STR×4%, 1D6+1), 3 flail (DEX×3%, 1D6), 4 mace (20%, 1D6+2), 5 morning-star flail (DEX×2%, 1D10+1), 6 sickle ((DEX+STR)%, 1D6+1). Armor: the group shares one `1D6` value. Needs triggering; then fights to the death. SAN 1D6.

### Mummy
`STR 20-22 · CON 15-17 · SIZ 10-11 · INT 10-11 · POW 15-16 · DEX 7` — **HP 13-14, Move 6**. Fist 70% (1D6+1D4); any weapon except firearms 50% (as weapon +1D6); minimum attack DEX×5. Armor 2 + any worn. Move Quietly 50%. No true POW (cannot replace spent POW); must be hacked apart. Its stench may itself disturb a viewer. SAN 1D8.

### Vampire
`STR ×2` (20-22) · `CON 13 · SIZ 10-11 · INT 10-11 · POW 13 · DEX 10-11 · CHA 10-11` — **HP 12, Move species + 2**. Touch 50% (1D4+1D4; reaches through armor to attack POW — a win drains the victim's POW into the vampire's, max POW 21), Bite 50% (1D4; penetrating → 1D6 STR/blood drain per round until victim dead or vampire destroyed). Hypnotic gaze: POW vs POW (resist on POW×5). Twice human strength; the cross repels; turns to smoke at will and at 0 HP (regaining 1 CON/round) — but if reduced **exactly** to 0 by a head hit it falls and a stake kills it. No special powers in daylight; may be bound to its grave-soil by day. SAN 1D6.

### Werewolf
`STR ×2` in wolf form (10-11 / 22) · `CON 10-11 · SIZ 10-11 · INT 10-11 (5-6 as wolf) · POW 10-11 · DEX 10-11 · CHA 10-11 (1)` — **HP 10-11, Move 8 (12)**. Bite 30% (1D8+1D4). Armor 1. Damaged only by silver weapons or fire. Track by Smell 60%, Hide 50%. (Two types: the cursed unwilling human, and the willing fiend.) SAN 1D8.

### Pixie
`STR 5 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 3-4 · INT 10-11 · POW 13 · DEX 14 · CHA 10-11` — **HP 7, Move 3 / 10 fly**. Sling 30% (1D8). Naturally invisible (visible only while attacking); spends its time playing pranks. Move Quietly 50%, Spot Hidden 40%, Practical Joke 80%. SAN 1D3.
