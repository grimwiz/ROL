Unlike the separable setting of some games, the 1920s is mechanically part of *Call of Cthulhu*: equipment, prices, money, and travel times are consulted in play and at character creation. Period weapon stats are in the Melee/Firearms and New Weapons tables in `05-combat.md`.

## The 1920s at the Table

The default era is the United States, circa 1920 — Prohibition, gangsters, early automobiles and aircraft, steamships and railroads for long travel, telegraph and telephone for distance communication. Globe-trotting investigations are normal; the world is reachable but slow. Investigators are ordinary people of the decade, not action heroes.

## Money and the Economy

- The dollar is convertible to gold; gold and silver trade as commodities and foreign currencies can be pegged to the gold price. Inflation runs ~1% per year across the decade.
- **No sales tax.** Property tax is small and local. Federal income tax is ~5% per year only on income over $20,000.
- Banks are independent and **uninsured** — savings can be lost in a collapse. Any interest rate above ~3% signals a shaky bank.
- **Credit Rating** (skill) represents an investigator's financial standing: the chance to raise a loan, pass a bad cheque, or bluff past a demand for credentials. The character sheet tracks yearly income and savings; wealth is otherwise handled descriptively by the Keeper.

## Heavy and Black-Market Weapons

Machine guns, mortars, grenades, and field guns are illegal for civilians and bought only on the black market — with a 25% chance per purchase that the goods prove undeliverable, the money is stolen, or the police intervene. Stats are in the New Weapons table (`05-combat.md`); representative black-market prices:

- Thompson SMG: `1D10 × $50 + 150` (one); `1D6 × $50` each in lots of 20+.
- .30 cal MG: `1D100 × $15 + 300`; ammo belt (500 rounds) $100.
- .50 cal MG: `1D100 × $30 + 400`.
- Mortar: `1D6 × $200`; shells $2 each (1/3 duds).
- Dynamite: $2 per stick (if legal purchase refused).
- Hand grenades: $50 per box of 24 (40% duds).
- 75mm field gun: `1D100 × $100 + 800`; shells $10 each (half duds).
