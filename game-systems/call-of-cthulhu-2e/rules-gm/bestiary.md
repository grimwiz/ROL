GM-facing reference. Knowing a creature's specifics is itself a Mythos matter (see the Cthulhu Mythos skill, `04-skills.md`); describe, don't name, at the table (`keeper-procedures.md`).

**Reading a stat block.** Characteristics are the average for the type (Keepers may roll the source dice for variation). Attacks list Weapon — Attk% (damage). Deities cannot be truly killed: at 0 HP they are dispelled and reform. SAN entries give the loss on a **failed** roll (and the smaller loss on a **success**). HP = (SIZ+CON)÷2; Move 8 = human speed; a second Move figure is flying/swimming/burrowing. The five power classes are in `mythos-lore-and-tomes.md`.

## Outer Gods and Elder Gods

### Azathoth, the Daemon Sultan (Outer God)
The blind idiot god at the centre of the universe; a "monstrous nuclear chaos." Summoned only by accident or madness.
`CON 300 · POW 100 · INT 0`, STR/DEX N/A, SIZ Variable — **HP 300, Move 0**.
- Arrives with a flautist Servitor and `1D10−1` lesser Other Gods. Each round (after all others act) lashes out with `1D6` pseudopods; hit chance by number used: 1→100%, 2→50%, 3→33%, 4→25%, 5→20%, 6→16%. Pseudopod damage `1D100`; cannot be Dodged; destroys structures wholesale.
- Once angered, grows: pseudopod reach 50 yards, doubling each round; the area is ruined to that radius.
- **Armor:** none, but cannot be slain (always returns to 300 HP). Striking an Elder Sign destroys the sign and deals Azathoth 3D6.
- **SAN:** 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Nodens, Lord of the Great Abyss (Elder God)
An aged, hoary deity who rides a seashell chariot; commands nightgaunts; at times almost friendly to humanity.
`STR 42 · CON 45 · SIZ 15 · INT 70 · POW 100 · DEX 21 · APP 21` — **HP 30, Move 12**.
- Never attacks physically himself; summons nightgaunts, or dismisses a foe (the foe rolls POW or less to resist; if it resists, Nodens departs, sometimes taking a favoured human part-way). Staff 100% (4D6; ignores all armor) when pressed.
- **Spells:** calls `1D10` nightgaunts per MP. Heals himself 1 HP/MP; cloaks himself in armor at 1 point/MP until moonset/sunrise.
- **SAN:** no loss.

### Nyarlathotep, the Crawling Chaos (Outer God)
Messenger and soul of the Other Gods; the only one with a true personality; a thousand forms (human "Black Man," clawed red-tentacled monster, winged tri-lobed eye). Seeks madness for humanity.
Human form: `STR 12 · CON 19 · SIZ 11 · INT 86 · POW 100 · DEX 19 · APP 18` — **HP 15, Move 12**. Monstrous: `STR 80 · CON 50 · SIZ 90 · INT 86 · POW 100 · DEX 19` — **HP 70, Move 16**.
- Human form: any weapon 100% (as weapon). Monstrous: Claw 85% (10D6).
- **Spells:** knows every spell; summons monsters at 1 MP per POW point (a shantak/hunting horror/Servitor for 1 MP).
- A slain human form bursts to release the monster, which departs. **SAN:** human form none; any other form 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Shub-Niggurath, the Black Goat of the Woods with a Thousand Young (Outer God)
A vast, festering cloudy mass that coalesces ropy tentacles, slime-dripping mouths, and hooved legs; a perverse fertility deity, widely worshipped.
`STR 72 · CON 170 · SIZ 120 · INT 21 · POW 70 · DEX 28` — **HP 145, Move 15**.
- Tentacle 100% (catch — held victims are dragged to her mouths and drained `1D6` STR/round permanently, helpless); Trample 15% (11D6, vs SIZ < 60, path 10-20 yards across). Against powerful foes she lands successive tentacles, adding a mouth each.
- **Armor:** none, but her mist body is immune to physical weapons (magic/fire/electricity harm normally); heals 2 HP per MP spent. Leaves at 145 damage.
- **Spells:** all Other God spells + Create Gate, Dread Curse of Azathoth, Voorish Sign. **SAN:** 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Yog-Sothoth, the All-in-One (Outer God)
A shifting conglomeration of iridescent spheres in the interstices between planes; coterminous with all time and space — the Key and the Gate. Patron of sorcerers (as Tawil at'Umr he safely grants travel).
`CON 400 · SIZ Variable · INT 40 · POW 100 · DEX 1`, STR N/A — **HP 400, Move 100**.
- Sphere touch 100% (`1D6` permanent CON loss, possibly APP); Silver bolt 80% (1D6 MP; death to all in a 5-yard circle, range ½ mile). Transports one character/round anywhere in time/space (unwilling victims resist on POW×3).
- **Armor:** none, but only enchanted weapons harm; dispelled at 0 HP.
- **Spells:** all. **SAN:** as Tawil at'Umr none; as the spheres 1D10 (1 on success); other forms 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Lesser Other Gods
The minor, mostly-mindless company that dances for Azathoth; powers and attacks vary by the Keeper.
`STR 42 · CON ~70 · SIZ ~100 · INT 0 · POW ~50 · DEX 5-6` — **HP ~85, Move 0-7**.
- Smash 60% (8D6). **Armor:** usually none; dispelled at 0 HP. **Spells:** summon at least one servitor each. **SAN:** 1D20 each (1 on success).

## Great Old Ones

### Cthugha (Great Old One)
An ever-shifting burning mass near Fomalhaut; served by fire vampires.
`STR 80 · CON 120 · SIZ 140 · INT 28 · POW 42 · DEX 21` — **HP 130, Move 0**.
- Arrives with `1D100×10` fire vampires; the area (2D10×20 yards across) is set ablaze — each round a victim rolls CON×5 or flees; on a failure takes 1 point/round automatically.
- Pseudopod 40% (1D6+13D6); Flame burst 60% (range 150 yards; CON vs POW or take POW in damage, half on a success; armor does not help).
- **Armor:** none, but hand weapons take 14 damage each strike (destroyed weapons deal nothing); bullets lose 14 points of damage.
- **SAN:** 1D20 (1D3 on success).

### Great Cthulhu (Great Old One)
The octopus-headed, winged, mountainous high priest of his race, entombed dreaming in sunken R'lyeh; most widely worshipped of the Great Old Ones.
`STR 140 · CON 110 · SIZ 210 · INT 42 · POW 42 · DEX 21` — **HP 160, Move 24 / 20 swim / 16 fly**.
- Claw 100% (1D6+21D6); facial Tentacle 100% (11D6). Each round scoops up `1D3` victims in his claws (4 with his tentacles); escape is by Dodge/Luck, not by fighting.
- **Armor:** ~21 points; regenerates 6 HP/round. Reduced to 0 HP (or after 160 damage) he dissolves into green mist and reforms at full HP in `1D10+10` minutes (immune to physical attack while reforming).
- **Spells:** all except Summon/Bind Nightgaunt and Contact Nodens. **SAN:** 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Father Dagon and Mother Hydra (rulers of the Deep Ones)
Deep ones grown enormous with age (each 30+ feet); active rulers of their race. Identical stats.
`STR 52 · CON 50 · SIZ 60 · INT 21 · POW 30 · DEX 20` — **HP 55, Move 10**.
- Claw 80% (1D6+6D6). **Armor:** 6-point skin. **Spells:** all summoning/binding of the lesser servitor races. **SAN:** 1D10 (1 on success).

### Hastur the Unspeakable, He Who is Not to be Named (Great Old One)
Dwells near Aldebaran; tied to the Lake of Hali, the Yellow Sign, and Carcosa; served by the byakhee. Worshipped by the Tcho-Tcho.
`STR 120 · CON 200 · SIZ 100 · INT 15 · POW 35 · DEX 30` — **HP 150, Move 20 / 50 fly**.
- Tentacle/Claw 100% (Death). Summoned only at night: each round 3 individuals within 20 yards must Dodge or be grasped and destroyed the following round. Must leave when Aldebaran sets; banished at 0 HP.
- **Armor:** 30 (thick scaly hide). **Spells:** Summon/Bind Byakhee, all Call and Contact spells, Brew Space-Mead. **SAN:** 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Ithaqua the Windwalker, the Wendigo (Great Old One)
Stalks the arctic north, carrying off travellers who are later found frozen in agony, body parts missing. Served by the invisible Lloigor.
`STR 50 · CON 150 · SIZ 100 · INT 10 · POW 35 · DEX 30` — **HP 125, Move 10 / 100 fly**.
- Air Gust 100% (lift and drop `1D10×10` feet — `1D6` damage per 10 feet; attacking several victims divides his STR among them on the Resistance Table); Claw 80% (6D6, ignores all armor).
- **Armor:** 10-point skin/slime. **Spells:** all Call and Contact. Dispersed at 125 damage (gone for the night).
- **SAN:** hearing his howl 1D6 (1 on success); seeing him 1D100 (1D10 on success).

### Nyogtha, the Thing That Should Not Be (Great Old One)
A blob of living darkness in underground caverns; teaches spells to witches for sacrifice and POW.
`STR 85 · CON 40 · SIZ 80 · INT 20 · POW 28 · DEX 20` — **HP 60, Move 10**.
- Tentacle 100% (1D10, or entrap and grapple); a strike-for-damage hits everyone within 10 yards for 1D10.
- **Armor:** ignores the first 10 points of damage each round; dispelled after 60 points. **Spells:** all Call and Contact, plus Create Gate. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D6 on success).

### Shudde M'ell, the Burrower Beneath (Great Old One)
Largest and most evil of the chthonians; resembles an outsized member of his race.
`STR 90 · CON 80 · SIZ 120 · INT 20 · POW 35 · DEX 15` — **HP 100, Move 8 / 2 burrow**.
- `1D8`-style Tentacles 100% (6D6 + 1D6 CON drain per tentacle, permanent); Crush 90% (12D6 to all within a 12-yard radius). Opens a `1D10+10`-yard hole that swallows everything above it.
- Alone causes a Richter-3.5 quake within ½ mile (stronger with other chthonians). **Armor:** 8 + regen 5/round. Driven off at 100 damage.
- **Spells:** all lesser-entity spells of the Great Old Ones. **SAN:** as a chthonian — 1D20 (1D3 on success).

### Tsathoggua (Great Old One)
A fat, furry, toad-headed god from Saturn dwelling in lightless N'Kai; served by his Formless Spawn; less malevolent than most, but terrible.
`STR 50 · CON 120 · SIZ 30 · INT 30 · POW 35 · DEX 27` — **HP 75, Move 24**.
- Roughly 50-50 he is sated or hungry. If hungry: Tentacle 100% (catch); a caught victim is drained 1 point from **every** characteristic each round (acid/vitriol/punctures), recovered only by 1 point per characteristic per month of hospital care.
- **Armor:** kinetic weapons cannot really harm him (regen 30/round, sealing); fire/electricity normal. Retreats at 75 damage. **Spells:** summons all lesser races except nightgaunts; Contacts the higher gods. **SAN:** 1D10 (no loss on success).

### Y'golonac (Great Old One)
A bloated, glowing, headless figure with mouths in its palms, walled up underground; comes when its name is read or spoken near evil; possesses the debased.
`STR 25 · CON 125 · SIZ 25 · INT 30 · POW 28 · DEX 14` — **HP 75, Move 10**.
- Touch 100% (psychic attack — victim rolls POW×5 or loses 1 INT and 1 POW each round until freed or consumed); Devour 100% (1D4 damage that never heals naturally).
- **Armor:** none. Departs at 75 damage. **Spells:** all Summon, Bind, and Contact. **SAN:** seeing the transformation 1D20 (1 on success).

### Yig, Father of Serpents (Great Old One)
An American serpent-god; worshipped by Amerinds, voodoo doctors, and the serpent people; his curse is madness and malformed children.
`STR 30 · CON 120 · SIZ 20 · INT 20 · POW 28 · DEX 18` — **HP 70, Move 10**.
- Hand 90% (2D6 crushing grasp; next round draws the victim in on STR vs STR to bite); Bite 95% (1D8 + instant death on penetration). Sends sacred snakes (giant white-crescent specimens; an untreatable bite) against those who offend him.
- **Armor:** 6-point scales; impaling weapons hurt only on an impale (then ignore armor); any weapon that penetrates takes 3D6 from his venomous blood. Dispelled at 70 damage. **Spells:** all Summon/Bind/Contact; adept at Contacting chthonians. **SAN:** 1D8 (no loss on success).

## Alien Races and Monsters

### Byakhee (Lesser Servitor Race)
Interstellar winged hybrids serving Hastur; vulnerable to ordinary weapons; summoned as steeds.
`STR 17-18 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 17-18 · INT 10-11 · POW 10-11 · DEX 13-14` — **HP 14-15, Move 5 / 20 fly**.
- Claw 35% (1D6+1D6); Bite 35% (1D6+1D6 + blood drain: −1D6 STR/round while attached until STR 0 = death; STR recovers by transfusion or 1 day/point).
- **Armor:** 2. **Spells:** POW 14+ has `1D4` (Hastur-related). **SAN:** 1D6 (1 on success).

### Chthonians (Greater Independent Race)
Immense slime-coated burrowing squid-worms; powerful telepaths; protective of walnut-sized young. Six life stages (instar table in source); adults withstand 4000°C.
Full adult: `STR 45-60 · CON 40-41 · SIZ 45-60 · INT 17-18 · POW 17-18 · DEX 7` — **HP 43-51, Move 6 / 1 burrow**.
- `1D8` Tentacles 75% (2D6-3D6 + blood drain: −1D6 CON/round, permanent; a draining tentacle reduces those that can strike). Or Crush 80% (5D6-6D6) over a circle SIZ/10 yards across.
- **Telepathy:** match POW vs POW to "bind" a victim to a site (MP cost scales with distance); victims resist daily. **Earthquakes:** combined POW ÷ 20 = Richter force (costs each that many MP); adults only.
- **Armor:** 5; regenerates 5 HP/round (not once slain). **Spells:** adults on INT×3, then `1D6`. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D3 on success); lesser instars 1D10 (1 on success); none for hatchlings.

### Dark Young of Shub-Niggurath (Greater Servitor Race)
12-20 ft writhing tree-like masses of ropy tentacles, mouths, and hooves; reek of the grave; found where Shub-Niggurath is worshipped.
`STR 44 · CON 16-17 · SIZ 44 · INT 14 · POW 17-18 · DEX 16-17` — **HP 30-31, Move 8**.
- Four Tentacles 80% (4D6 + STR drain: −1D3 STR/round permanently while held).
- **Armor:** none, but firearms/bullets do only 1 (2 on impale); shotguns do minimum; clubs/blades full. **Spells:** ½ INT. **Skills:** Sneak 60%, Hide in Woods 80%. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D3 on success).

### Deep Ones (Lesser Servitor Race)
Immortal amphibious fish-frog humanoids serving Cthulhu and Dagon/Hydra; interbreed with coastal humans (hybrids transform at age `1D20+20`).
`STR 14 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 16-17 · INT 13 · POW 10-11 · DEX 10-11` — **HP 13-14, Move 8 / 10 swim**.
- Claw 25% (1D6+1D4); Trident 25% (1D6+1+1D4; impales, throwable). **Armor:** 1. **Spells:** POW 14+ knows `1D4`+. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Dholes (Greater Independent Race)
Hundreds-of-feet-long wormlike burrowers, not native to earth; dislike light.
`STR ~500 · CON ~150 · SIZ ~550 · INT 7 · POW 35 · DEX 2-3` — **HP ~350, Move 18 / 10 burrow**.
- Engulf 80% (swallows); Spit Goo 50% (range 2-3 miles; caustic slime — drowning checks + 1 damage/round ignoring armor; escape on STR×1).
- **Armor:** equal to POW. **Spells:** none. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D4 on success).

### Dimensional Shamblers (Lesser Independent Race)
Ape-and-insect things that walk between worlds; fade between planes (4 MP, one round, defenceless during it), carrying victims away forever (+1 MP per 10 SIZ).
`STR 19 · CON 16-17 · SIZ 19 · INT 7 · POW 10-11 · DEX 10-11` — **HP 18, Move 7**.
- Two Claws 30% (1D8+1D6). **Armor:** 3. **Spells:** INT 10+ knows 1 per INT over 9. **SAN:** 1D10 (no loss on success).

### Fire Vampires (Lesser Servitor Race)
Living points of flame serving Cthugha; ignite what they touch.
`CON 7 · SIZ 1 · INT 10-11 · POW 13 · DEX 16-17`, STR N/A — **HP 7, Move 11**.
- Touch 85% (2D6 fire — heat vs CON, full damage if heat wins, half if resisted; armor ignored). Same attack drains `1D10` MP if its MP beat the victim's.
- **Armor:** none, but immune to material weapons; water does 1/half-gallon, an extinguisher 1D6, sand 1D3. **Spells:** POW 17+ has `1D3`. **SAN:** none.

### Flying Polyps (Greater Independent Race)
Half-material, partly-invisible whistling horrors that command wind; sealed in ancient wells; exterminated the Great Race.
`STR 50 · CON 25 · SIZ 50 · INT 14 · POW 16-17 · DEX 13` — **HP 38, Move 8 / 12 fly**, Damage Bonus 5D6 (windblast only).
- `2D6` Tentacles 85% (1D10, ignores armor). **Windblast** (5D6 to a 10-yd area, −1D6 per range increment). **Capturing wind** (STR vs ½ POW to halt a victim). **Windstorms** with their fellows. Each wind power costs 1 MP/turn.
- **Armor:** 4 + near-immunity to physical weapons (a 2D6+3 gun does 5, then −4 armor); enchanted weapons/heat/electricity full. Invisible at will (1 MP/turn; locate by Listen at −50% to hit); when visible, POW subtracted from chance to hit. **Spells:** roll `1D20`; if ≤ INT, knows that many. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D3 on success).

### Formless Spawn of Tsathoggua (Lesser Servitor Race)
Amorphous black slime that flows through cracks and takes shapes at will; found in Tsathoggua's temples.
`STR 9-27 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 15-33 · INT 13 · POW 10-11 · DEX 19` — **HP 13-22, Move 12**.
- Whip 90% (1D6, reach DEX yards, `1D3+1` attacks); Tentacle 60% (1D6-3D6, reach SIZ yards, hits `1D3` foes); Bite 30% (swallows — 1 damage round 1, +1 each round); Bludgeon 20% (2D6-6D6). One mode per round.
- **Armor:** none, but immune to all physical weapons (wounds seal); fire/chemicals/spells affect them. **Spells:** roll INT+POW or less. **SAN:** 1D10 (1 on success).

### Ghouls (Lesser Independent Race)
Rubbery, dog-faced grave-dwellers in tunnels beneath cities; a human can slowly become one.
`STR 16-17 · CON 13 · SIZ 13 · INT 13 · POW 13 · DEX 13` — **HP 13, Move 9**.
- Two Claws 30% (1D6+1D4) + Bite 30% (1D6+1D4; on a hit, worries the victim — automatic bite each later round while grappled, both at +20% to hit; dislodge on STR vs STR).
- **Armor:** none, but guns do half. **Spells:** roll ≤ INT for that many. **Skills:** Sneak 80%, Hide 60%, Listen 70%, Spot Hidden 50%, Climb 85%, Jump 75%. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Great Race of Yith (Lesser Independent Race)
Ten-foot iridescent cones (alien minds in earth-born bodies) who conquered time by mind-exchange; civilised, avoid melee; fight with lightning-guns. Met (in cone form) only via time travel.
`STR 42 · CON 26 · SIZ 64 · INT 20 · POW 13 · DEX 10` — **HP 45, Move 7**.
- Two Pincers 40% (1D6+6D6); Lightning Gun 30% (1D10/charge, 32 charges; over 4 at once risks burnout; base range 100 yards). **Armor:** 8.
- **Spells:** rarely; roll ≤ INT for `1D3`. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Hounds of Tindalos (Greater Independent Race)
Lean, athirst things from the angles of time; pursue any victim who has glimpsed them across time. Manifest only through corners of 120° or less.
`STR 16-17 · CON 30-31 · SIZ 16-17 · INT 17-18 · POW 24-25 · DEX 10-11` — **HP 23-24, Move 6 / 40 fly**.
- Paw 90% (1D6+1D6 + living "ichor" doing poison damage of 2D6 potency each round until wiped off on DEX×5); Tongue 90% (a painless hole; `1D3` permanent POW drain, no HP damage). One attack per round.
- Travel time to a victim = (years between) ÷ 100,000,000 days. **Armor:** 2 + regen 4/round; mundane weapons do no harm (need enchanted). **Spells:** `1D8`. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D3 on success).

### Hunting Horrors (Greater Servitor Race)
40-ft ropy black winged serpents that hunt for the gods (especially Nyarlathotep); dispelled by daylight.
`STR 29-30 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 41-42 · INT 15-16 · POW 21 · DEX 13-14` — **HP 26, Move 7 / 11 fly**.
- Bite 65% (1D6+3D6); Tail 90% (grapple only — held on STR vs STR; a grappled victim is bitten at +20% and cannot act). **Armor:** 9; cannot be impaled by bullets.
- **Spells:** roll `1D20`; if ≤ INT, knows that many. **SAN:** 1D10 (no loss on success).

### Mi-Go, the Fungi from Yuggoth (Lesser Independent Race)
Pinkish winged crustacean-fungi mining rare ores in the mountains; worship Nyarlathotep and Shub-Niggurath; fly through space (clumsy in air); dissolve a few hours after death; unphotographable by ordinary plates.
`STR 10-11 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 10-11 · INT 13 · POW 13 · DEX 14` — **HP 10-11, Move 7 / 9 fly**.
- Two Nippers 30% (1D6 + grapple — break free on STR vs STR, else flown off and dropped or taken to bursting altitude).
- **Armor:** none, but impaling weapons do minimum damage. **Spells:** INT×2 chance for `1D3`; also brain-canister surgery (keeps a living human brain in a metal tube). **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Nightgaunts (Lesser Servitor Race)
Faceless, horned, barb-tailed black fliers that serve Nodens; grab intruders and drop them in horrible places.
`STR 10-11 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 14 · INT 3-4 · POW 10-11 · DEX 13` — **HP 13, Move 6 / 12 fly**.
- Grapple 30% (STR vs STR — two or more may combine STR); Tickle 30% (only on a grappled foe; CON×3 or be immobilised `1D6` rounds, 1 round even on success; reaches through clothes/armor).
- **Armor:** 2. **Spells:** none. **Skills:** Sneak 90%, Hide 90%. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Old Ones / Elder Things (Lesser Independent Race)
Barrel-bodied, winged, starfish-headed beings who seeded earthly life and built an Antarctic city; warred with shoggoths and Mi-Go.
`STR 38 · CON 22-23 · SIZ 28 · INT 15-16 · POW 10-11 · DEX 16-17` — **HP 25-26, Move 8 / 10 fly**.
- Five Tentacles 40% (3D3 constriction; once gripping, clings and crushes each round). **Armor:** 7-point skin. **Spells:** chance INT×3 to know `1D4`. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Sand-Dwellers (Lesser Servitor Race)
Sand-encrusted, koala-faced cave-dwellers of the American Southwest serving the Great Old Ones; emerge at night.
`STR 10-11 · CON 13 · SIZ 16-17 · INT 10-11 · POW 10-11 · DEX 13` — **HP 15, Move 8**.
- Two Claws 30% (1D6+1D4). **Armor:** 3-point hide. **Spells:** POW 14+ knows `1D8`. **Skills:** Hide 60%, Sneak 50%, Spot Hidden 50%. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Serpent People (Lesser Independent Race)
Upright serpent-folk, prehistoric sorcerers and scientists, near-extinct; some disguised among humans.
`STR 10-11 · CON 10-11 · SIZ 10-11 · INT 16-17 · POW 13 · DEX 13` — **HP 10-11, Move 8**.
- Bite 35% (1D8 + poison of potency = the serpent's CON); Weapon 35% (as weapon; may bite simultaneously). **Armor:** 1-point scales.
- **Spells:** modern serpent men know 2D6+; a common spell disguises them as human. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Servitors of the Other Gods (Greater Servitor Race)
Amorphous frog/squid demon-flautists who pipe for the Outer Gods and summon beings.
`STR 14 · CON 16-17 · SIZ 20 · INT 17-18 · POW 19 · DEX 16-17` — **HP 18-19, Move 7**.
- `2D3` Tentacles each round, 45% (2D6; each = twice damage bonus, min 1D6).
- **Armor:** none, but immune to normal weapons (magical do normal); regenerates 3 HP/round. **Spells:** at least `1D10`, plus summon `2D6−6` being-types by piping (1 MP each, +1 MP per 5 rounds present). **SAN:** 1D10 (1 on success).

### Shantaks (Lesser Servitor Race)
Elephant-sized, horse-headed, scaled fliers used as steeds by the Other Gods' servants; terrified of nightgaunts; can fly to Azathoth's throne.
`STR 34 · CON 13 · SIZ 50 · INT 3-4 · POW 10-11 · DEX 10` — **HP 32, Move 6 / 30 fly**.
- Bite 55% (1D6+4D6). **Armor:** 9-point hide. **Spells:** none. **SAN:** 1D6 (no loss on success).

### Shoggoths (Lesser Servitor Race)
Vast protoplasmic black bubbles with shifting eyes, crying "Tekeli-li!"; amphibious former slaves of the Old Ones, ever more intelligent and rebellious.
`STR 63 · CON 42 · SIZ 84 · INT 7 · POW 10-11 · DEX 3-4` — **HP 63, Move 10 rolling**.
- Crush 100% (8D6; covers 5×5 yards — each target matches STR vs the shoggoth's STR or is sucked in for damage-bonus damage each round; STR is divided among multiple targets).
- **Armor:** none, but fire/electricity do half; physical weapons do only 1 (even on impale); regen 2/round. **Spells:** none normally. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D6 on success).

### Star-Spawn of Cthulhu (Greater Servitor Race)
Smaller kin of Cthulhu — octopoid beings dwelling in deep ocean trenches and among the stars.
`STR 70 · CON 45-60 · SIZ 90-120 · INT 21 · POW 21 · DEX 10-11` — **HP 68-90, Move 20 / 20 swim**.
- `1D4` Tentacles 80% (9D3-11D3); or Claw 80% (9D6-11D6). **Armor:** 10 + regen 3/round.
- **Spells:** at least `3D6`. **SAN:** 1D20 (1D6 on success).

### Star Vampires (Lesser Independent Race)
Invisible, blood-drinking jellies with taloned tentacles, betrayed only by a ghoulish tittering until they feed and turn red.
`STR 26 · CON 13 · SIZ 26 · INT 10-11 · POW 15-16 · DEX 9-10` — **HP 20, Move 6 / 9 fly**.
- `1D4` Talons 40% (1D6+2D6, grasp and squeeze); Bite 80% (1D6 STR/blood drain per round).
- **Armor:** 4 + invisibility (firing by sound is at −50% to hit; half in close combat; visible for `1D6` rounds after feeding); bullets do half. **Spells:** INT×3 chance for `1D3`. **SAN:** 1D10 (1 on success).
