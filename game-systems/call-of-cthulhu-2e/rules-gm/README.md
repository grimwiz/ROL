# Call of Cthulhu — Keeper Rulebook

The complete edition: every rule a Keeper needs, including the Mythos material withheld from the player book.

## Shared mechanics (identical to `../rules-player/`)

`00-system-overview` · `01-characteristics` · `02-core-resolution` · `03-character-creation` · `04-skills` · `05-combat` · `06-damage-and-healing` · `07-sanity` · `09-beasts-and-creatures` · `10-equipment-and-the-1920s` · `11-the-1920s-reference`

(`09-beasts-and-creatures` and `11-the-1920s-reference` are non-spoiler period/creature reference, so they appear in both books.)

## Keeper-only material

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `08-magic-and-mythos.md` | Full magic chapter: the Cthulhu Mythos skill, Magic Points, casting, the **Books of the Cthulhu Mythos** table, spell categories, the **Complete Listing of Spells**, notable spell effects, Increasing POW. |
| `bestiary.md` | Mythos creature stat blocks (deities, Great Old Ones, alien races). |
| `keeper-procedures.md` | Setting up and running scenarios and campaigns; tone, pacing, escalation. |
| `mythos-lore-and-tomes.md` | The deities and races, the five power classes, Spells-for-Monsters, per-spell components, Gate distances. |

> The `08`, `bestiary`, and Mythos files contain spoilers — keep them on the Keeper's side. Player copies of the shared mechanics live in `../rules-player/`; a fix to those must be applied in both books.

Scenario-facing material (case seeds, the historical 1920s reference, the Sourcebook's generic "Beasts & Monsters", and the two bundled adventures as cases) is **not** part of the rulebook and will be handled separately.
