## The Shape of the Mythos

- **The Outer Gods** (also Other Gods, Elder Gods) rule the universe and have little to do with humanity except through Nyarlathotep, their messenger and soul. Most are blind and idiotic. **Azathoth**, the daemon sultan, writhes mindlessly at the centre of all things; **Yog-Sothoth**, co-ruler, is coterminous with all time and space but locked outside the mundane universe and reached only by mighty spells. Their associated races (shantaks, hunting horrors, Servitors of the Other Gods, the spawn of Shub-Niggurath) are the rarest monsters.
- **The Great Old Ones** are immensely powerful alien beings — not true gods — each independent, many imprisoned in some way until "the stars are right." **Cthulhu**, entombed in sunken R'lyeh, is the most prominent on earth and by far the most worshipped. They are served by races dwelling in earth's remoter places, and their cults are the foe the investigators most often face.
- **Elder Gods** are a neutral, possibly rival group; only **Nodens** is named, and he is sometimes almost kindly.
- **Alien races** filled earth's deep past: the Old Ones (flew here in the Cambrian, bred earthly life and the shoggoths that destroyed them), the Great Race of Yith (mind-travellers, exterminated by the flying polyps ~50 million years ago), Cthulhu's spawn, the serpent folk (Permian), and the Mi-Go (mining colonies since the Jurassic). Today only a few remain active — the Deep Ones beneath the sea and the Mi-Go in the mountains; others sleep or visit rarely.

## Summoning Spells — Components

General rule: 1 MP per 10% chance of success (except Summon Servitor of the Outer Gods, triple cost), ≥5 minutes chanting per MP, max useful 10 MP (a roll of 96-00 still fails). The being arrives within `2D10` minutes; the caster loses `1D3` SAN. Most require a material component:

| Spell | Component / condition |
|---|---|
| Summon Byakhee | A whistle (enchantable, +10% per POW spent); night, when Aldebaran is up (Oct-Mar). |
| Summon Dark Young | Blood sacrifice of a beast SIZ 8+, cut with a knife; outdoors in a wood; dark of the moon. |
| Summon Dimensional Shambler | A dagger of pure unalloyed metal (enchantable, +10% per POW). Castable anytime. |
| Summon Fire Vampire | A bonfire/flame; night, when Fomalhaut is up (Sep-Nov best). |
| Summon Hunting Horror | Blood sacrifice of a sentient being (taken by the horror; if none, it takes the caster); night, open air. |
| Summon Nightgaunt | A stone bearing the Elder Sign (not star-shaped); night, moon below the horizon. |
| Summon Servitor of the Outer Gods | An especially unhallowed night (Mayday, Midsummer, Halloween, Walpurgis); a flute (enchantable). Triple cost (3 MP per 10%). |
| Summon Star Vampire | A book inscribed with the spell (enchantable); cloudless night. |

## Binding Spells

One Bind spell per summonable being, specific to type. Spend a round chanting + 1 MP, then match MP vs the creature's MP on the Resistance Table; success binds it to obey one order, after which it is free. A failed bind makes it attack. A not-yet-arrived summoned being can be bound in advance. A being already bound by another, or more than 100 yards off, cannot be bound.

## Call and Dismiss Deity Spells

**Call:** the group chants 1 full minute per 1% of success; total chance = sum of MP spent. Costs `1D10` SAN.

| Call spell | Component / condition |
|---|---|
| Call Azathoth | Night, outdoors; no special prep, but dangerous. |
| Call Shub-Niggurath | A blood-consecrated stone altar (200 SIZ of victims to consecrate, 40 SIZ each later use) in a wood; dark of the moon. Adds +20% to Summon Dark Young; +10% per Dark Young present. |
| Call Yog-Sothoth | A 10-yard stone tower (enchantable, +5% per POW); a human sacrifice each casting; open area, cloudless sky. |
| Call Cthugha | A moving flame; night, when Fomalhaut is up (Sep-Nov). |
| Call Hastur | Nine enchanted stones in a "V" (1 POW each, ≥9 cubic yards each); clear night, Aldebaran up (Oct-Mar). Adds +30% to Summon Byakhee; +10% per byakhee present. |
| Call Ithaqua | A snow mound to stand on; far north, below freezing. |
| Call Nyogtha | At an entrance to an extensive cavern connecting to Nyogtha's dwelling. |

**Dismiss:** contained within each Call spell. Costs 1 MP per 5 POW (or fraction) of the deity, plus 1 MP per 5% chance of dismissal. Castable anytime, anywhere; no SAN cost.

## Contact Spells

Every Contact spell works the same way (whether the target is a race or a named deity): it takes a minute or two to cast and costs the magic points listed for that spell. The contacted being does not arrive at once — roll `1D100` for the hours until it comes, and it tends to appear when the caller is alone or among cultists/friends. Members of brave races (Old Ones, sand-dwellers, mi-go) may come in a small group. Knowing one Contact spell never helps cast another. Each named deity's manner of answering (a dream from Cthulhu, a projection of Tsathoggua, possession by Y'golonac, etc.) is given with that being.
