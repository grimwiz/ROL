Mythos magic is not the wizardry of fantasy: it is the manipulation of alien forces, learned at the cost of Sanity. Specific spell write-ups and which beings know which spells are in `mythos-lore-and-tomes.md`; creature stat blocks are in `bestiary.md`.

## The Cthulhu Mythos Skill

- Base 0%; maximum 98% for a player-character.
- Never chosen at creation and never raised by experience or skill points.
- Gained only by insane insight (`07-sanity.md`) or by reading Mythos tomes (below). Increases are permanent and cannot be forgotten.
- Caps maximum SAN at `99 − Cthulhu Mythos`.
- A successful roll identifies a Mythos creature's class (or exact type if seen), a spell from watching it cast, or a tome from a page or two.

## Magic Points

- Magic Points (MP) start equal to POW.
- They regenerate at **¼ POW per 6 hours** (round down) — a full pool over a day. They never exceed POW; surplus MP from other sources can be spent but do not regenerate.
- At **0 MP** the character falls unconscious until MP returns to at least 1.

## Learning Spells

A spell may be learned by:

- **Reading a Mythos tome** — gain the book's Cthulhu Mythos bonus and, potentially, its spells; each tome costs SAN (table below).
- **Being taught** — one-on-one; each week roll INT or less on `D100` to learn.
- **A deity or monster** — via a tablet/scroll (learned like a book) or telepathic implant (retained on a successful Idea roll).

## Casting Spells

Five requirements must be met:

1. **Magic points** (or, for some spells, permanent **POW** — POW does not regenerate).
2. **Physical component** — reusable for some spells, consumed by others.
3. **Time** — from seconds to over a week.
4. **Incantation** — the caster needs freedom of movement and usually voice.
5. **Sanity** — most spells cost SAN automatically; no SAN roll reduces it.

## Spell Categories

| Category | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| **Summon** | 1 MP per 10% chance of success (max useful 10 MP; even at 10 a roll of 96-00 fails); ≥5 minutes chanting per MP. The being arrives within `2D10` minutes; the caster loses `1D3` SAN. |
| **Bind** | One Bind spell per summonable being. Spend 1 MP and match MP vs the creature's MP on the Resistance Table; success binds it to obey one order, then it is free. A failed bind makes it attack. |
| **Contact** | Cast in a minute or two; a member of the contacted race — or the named deity — answers, arriving after `1D100` hours (brave races may come in a small group). The effect is the same for every Contact spell; knowing one never helps cast another. MP cost is set per spell. |
| **Call** (deity) | The whole group chants 1 full minute per 1% of success (sum of MP spent = % chance); the deity arrives. Costs `1D10` SAN. |
| **Dismiss** (deity) | Contained within each Call spell. Costs 1 MP per 5 POW (or fraction) of the deity, plus 1 MP per 5% chance of dismissal. Castable anytime, anywhere; no SAN cost. |

## Increasing POW

When a character succeeds at a spell that matched his MP or POW against a target on the Resistance Table, POW may rise: chance = `(21 − current POW) × 5%` to gain `1D3` POW.

Other routes: sacrifice 10 SAN for 1 POW (not possible at 9 or less SAN), or bargain POW from a deity (Keeper's call).

## Notable Spell Effects

- **Shrivelling** — 2 rounds to cast; blasts the victim for 1 point per MP invested if the caster overcomes the target's MP on the Resistance Table. SAN cost = ½ the MP spent.
- **Dread Curse of Azathoth** — match MP vs MP; the target permanently loses `1D3` POW. Costs the speaker `1D6` SAN and 4 MP.
- **Powder of Ibn-Ghazi** — reveals invisible things (creatures, magical lines, Gate auras) for ~10 heartbeats; no SAN to use, but seeing what it exposes may cost SAN.
- **Voorish Sign** — a gesture that adds +5% to any accompanying spell; costs 1 MP and 1 SAN.
- **Elder Sign** — seals an opening against the Great Old Ones, Outer Gods, and their minions; making one permanently sacrifices 2 POW (no SAN).
- **Create Gate / travel** — costs POW by distance (see the Sample Locales table in `mythos-lore-and-tomes.md`); no SAN to create, 1 SAN per use.
- **Resurrection** — reduces a complete corpse to essential salts and restores it; 3 MP and `1D10` SAN per use; the reverse formula dissolves the returnee.
- **Enchant Item** — several distinct spells, each enchanting a device; each costs a blood sacrifice, 1+ POW, `1D4` SAN, and at least a day.
