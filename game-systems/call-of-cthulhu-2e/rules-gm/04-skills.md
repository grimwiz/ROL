## Improving Skills (Experience)

- When an investigator uses a skill **successfully** during play, the Keeper may let the player tick that skill. Only success teaches; one tick per skill per adventure, regardless of how often it was used.
- After the adventure, roll `D100` for each ticked skill. If the roll is **higher** than the current skill, raise it by `1D6%`. If equal to or under, no gain.
- The **Cthulhu Mythos** skill is never improved this way.

## Skill Notes

Mechanically significant skills (paraphrased):

- **Bargain** — lower a price. For each 2% the offered price is below the asking price, subtract 1% from the roll. The seller never takes a loss.
- **Climb** — roll every 10-30 feet by difficulty. To climb quietly, the one roll is read against both Climb and Sneak; succeeding Climb but failing Sneak means you climb but make noise.
- **Credit Rating** — chance to get a loan, pass a bad cheque, or bluff past a demand for credentials; the amount is the Keeper's call from social standing and apparent wealth.
- **Cthulhu Mythos** — base 0%; max 98% for a player; never chosen at creation and never raised by experience. Gained only through insane insight (`07-sanity.md`) or reading Mythos tomes (`08-magic-and-mythos.md`); permanent and unforgettable. Caps maximum SAN at `99 − Cthulhu Mythos`. A successful roll identifies a monster's general class from spoor (its exact type if seen), identifies a spell from watching it cast, or identifies a Mythos book from a page or two.
- **Debate / Oratory** — convince intellectually (Debate) or emotionally (Oratory); take ~5 minutes. Opposed debaters roll simultaneously until one fails while the other succeeds. Neither works on a mind already made up against the speaker.
- **Diagnose Disease** — identifies an ailment (or malingering); success doubles a subsequent Pharmacy or Treat Disease attempt on that ailment.
- **Dodge** — DEX×2 base; improvable by experience. A dodging character may take no other action that round (no attack).
- **Drive Automobile / Operate Heavy Machinery** — 25%+ drives safely under normal conditions; a roll is needed in a chase (opposed) or under bad conditions, or you wreck. Each machine type is a separate skill.
- **Fast Talk** — instant agreement (~1 minute); the target may shake it off with a later **Idea** roll. Useless on a made-up mind (use Debate/Oratory).
- **First Aid** — heals `1D3` damage; only one attempt per wound (success or fail). Also wakes the stunned, sets limbs, etc.
- **Hide + Sneak** — when both apply, make one roll read against each; you can pass one and fail the other. Firing from cover uses half Hide.
- **Jump** — leap up to your height vertically or twice horizontally; a successful Jump subtracts `1D6` from falling damage.
- **Law** — chance to know a relevant law; halved abroad unless you study `30 − INT` days of that nation's law.
- **Library Use** — find a reference (if it exists) in 4 hours of search; failure wastes the time. Does not bypass locked special collections.
- **Linguist** — speak any common language at Linguist/5; identify a speaker's language or true nationality (limited by your skill in the language spoken).
- **Mechanical Repair** — fix machines; can pick locks, usually needing tools (simple locks excepted).
- **Pick Pocket** — steal unobtrusively; halved for items in intimate contact (watch, necklace).
- **Pilot Aircraft** — roll for difficult flying and on every landing (doubled chance landing in good conditions); a failed landing usually only damages the craft. Each aircraft type is a separate skill. (No helicopters in the 1920s.)
- **Psychoanalysis** — eases a madman to near-normal for a day; over a prolonged period can cure insanity or raise SAN (see `07-sanity.md`).
- **Psychology** — tell if someone is psychotic; a second success names the ailment, or guesses desires/needs.
- **Read/Write (own language)** — EDU×5; roll only for very hard or archaic documents. No communication skill (Bargain/Debate/Fast Talk/Oratory) can exceed Read/Write when done in writing.
- **Read/Write Other Language** — chance to grasp each key point of a document; for a Mythos tome, one roll for the gist. Reading is never below ½ the Speak score.
- **Ride** — 25%+ rides at any gait; on a sudden event, stay mounted on a Ride roll; a thrown rider may roll Ride to land on his feet or take `1D6`.
- **Speak Other Language** — base INT×5 for the native tongue; conversation succeeds at twice the lesser speaker's skill. Never below ½ Read.
- **Spot Hidden** — notice secret doors, compartments, concealed ambushers, and similar.
- **Swim** — fail and you begin Drowning (see `06-damage-and-healing.md`); a drowning swimmer may roll each round to surface, then again to move.
- **Throw** — hit with a thrown object (correct part first); range one metre per STR point above the object's size. A failed throw scatters.
- **Track** — follow over soft ground; −10% per day of age, impossible over concrete or after rain.
- **Treat Disease** — eases mild or recurrent illness; in a hospital with drugs and care, even severe disease.
- **Treat Poison** — applied before symptoms, purges `2D6` levels of poison; also identifies the poison and best treatment.

Other listed skills (Accounting, Anthropology, Archaeology, Astronomy, Botany, Chemistry, Electrical Repair, Geology, History, Listen, Make Maps, Occult, Pharmacy, Photography, Sing, Zoology, Camouflage) work as their names imply, gated by the relevant successful roll.
