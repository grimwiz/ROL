# Character Creation

<!-- Source: rulebook ch.II p11-12 (background, money, equipment, CHA changes); appendix A p123 (background table). Status: extracted-draft. -->

## Procedure

1. **Roll the seven characteristics** — `3D6` each for a human (STR, CON, SIZ, INT, POW, DEX, CHA). Non-humans use the ranges in `11-monsters.md`.
2. **Derive attributes** (`01-characteristics.md`): the category modifiers (Attack, Parry, Defense, Perception, Stealth, Manipulation, Knowledge), Hit Points (CON ± SIZ/POW), the damage bonus (avg STR+SIZ), magic points (= POW), and **base strike rank** (from SIZ + DEX; weapon length is added in play — see `06-combat.md`).
3. **Roll background and starting money** (below).
4. **Take starting equipment** for that background.
5. **Spend the starting stake** on weapon/skill training and gear (`05-advancement.md`); record weapons, skills, and armour on the sheet.

Money is in **Lunars (L)**.

## Background and Starting Money

Roll `D100` on the Background Table, then follow the heritage rolled. The result sets starting money; it does **not** limit later advancement.

| D100 | Background | Starting money |
|---|---|---|
| 01-25 | Peasant | `D100` L |
| 26-60 | Townsman | `2D100` L |
| 61-85 | Barbarian | `D100` L * |
| 86-95 | Poor Noble | `D100×5` L / game year ** |
| 96-99 | Rich Noble | `D100×10` L / game year ** |
| 00 | Very Rich Noble | `D100×20` L / game year ** |

\* A barbarian also has a riding animal (20% chance not), leather armour, and basic weapons.
\*\* Noble income must be collected at a fixed spot each game year or it simply piles up (no interest). A poor noble's income is usually cut off around age 21; richer nobles generally keep drawing their stipend, at the referee's discretion.

## Starting Equipment

Every beginning character has: a **tunic**, **sandals/boots**, **underwear**, a **warm cloak/coat**, **headgear**, a **belt knife**, and **fire-making gear**. By background, additionally:

| Background | Adds |
|---|---|
| Peasant | snares, drinking skin, basic camp gear, torches |
| Townsman | flasks, torches, lamps, rope, assorted tools (mallets, spikes…) |
| Barbarian | snares, rope, torches, riding gear, a week's rations, cooking & camping gear |
| Poor Noble | a basic weapon (e.g. broadsword), ring-mail hauberk, open helm, plus a 2-handed or missile weapon (by DEX) |
| Rich / Very Rich Noble | a basic weapon, brigandine or chainmail hauberk, chainmail limb protection, closed helm, a riding horse, a 2-handed or missile weapon, and anything else affordable |

## Changing CHA After Creation

CHA shifts with deeds (referee's call), notably:

- Each **25% of Oratory** learned: +1 CHA (maximum +4).
- Each **25% with the main weapon above 50%**: +1 CHA (no limit).
- Owning **showy magical objects**: +1 CHA once (any number of items, still just +1).
- **Successful leadership** of an expedition: +1 CHA (roll CHA or less on `D100`, or referee's criterion).
- A **disastrous expedition**: the leader rolls CHA or less or loses `1–3` CHA.
