# Rune Magic and Cults

<!-- Source: rulebook ch.VII p53-72 (runes, cults, rune lords/priests, rune spells, divine intervention, elementals); divine intervention table p124. Status: extracted-draft. Specific cult write-ups (Orlanth, Kyger Litor, Black Fang) are setting material — summarised, not reproduced. -->

Rune magic is the magic of the gods, granted through cult membership. It is more powerful than battle magic (`08-magic.md`) but costly: a worshipper must permanently **sacrifice characteristic POW** to gain rune spells (the POW can be regained later by normal POW-gain rolls, `02-core-resolution.md`).

## Cult Ranks

- **Lay member** — a worshipper; access to the cult's common battle magic and festivals.
- **Initiate** — has sworn to the cult (join by rolling POW or less on `D100`). Gains: **divine intervention** once per week, **one-use** rune spells (sacrifice the spell's POW cost for a single casting), and training in the cult's skills.
- **Rune Lord** — a devotee/weapon-master with high characteristics (typically 90% in a cult weapon and several cult skills) and an **allied spirit**; a champion rather than a spellcaster.
- **Rune Priest** — a spellcaster with basic **POW ≥ 18**, who sacrifices POW for **reusable** rune spells. A priest pays ~90% of income to the temple, may not train STR/CON/DEX or DEX-based skills (only POW and CHA improve), and gains POW faster. He may take a leave of absence (≤ 1 game year) to train, and may become a high priest or a **rune priest-lord** once he holds 15+ sacrificed points of reusable rune magic.

Joining a second cult requires being an initiate/priest of one first and the permission of both; an associate priest buys abilities outright (1000 L per POW point of a specialty spell) and owes no tithe to the second cult.

## How Rune Magic Works

- A priest **sacrifices characteristic POW** (taken above 18) at a ceremony to learn a spell; the POW cost is listed per spell. No money is needed, only the POW.
- A rune spell **almost always works** (it is the god's power); it is cast in one action and **prevents casting any other rune or battle magic that round** (the sole exception is **Extension**, cast alongside the spell it extends).
- **Once cast, a reusable spell cannot be recast until regained** — sacrificed POW returns at **1 point per day**. Some spells (notably **Divine Intervention**) are **non-reusable** and must be sacrificed for again.
- **Stackable** spells can be sacrificed for at multiple points for greater effect; non-stackable ones cannot.
- The god grants extra potency with spells tied to its own **Rune** (element/power); spells common to most cults are the **Standard Rune Magic Spells**.

### Standard Rune Magic Spells

Common to most cults (POW cost / stackable):

| Spell | POW | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Divine Intervention | var | Stackable; non-reusable — beg the god to act directly. |
| Runepower | 1 / 2 / 3 | As per spell — boosts cult magic. |
| Spell Teaching | — | Non-stackable; lets a priest teach a spell. |
| Multispell | 2 / 3 | Non-stackable; cast two/three rune spells at once. |
| Extension | — | Cast alongside another spell to extend its duration. |
| Dismiss/Dispel (Magic) | — | Counter rune/battle magic. |

Each cult adds **specialty spells** tied to its Rune (e.g. weather, darkness, death). Cult-specific spell lists belong to each cult's write-up.

## Divine Intervention

An initiate (or priest) may, once per week in extremity, beg the god to act:

- Roll **current POW or less on `D100`**. On success the god intervenes and the character **permanently loses POW equal to the dice roll** (regainable). On failure nothing happens and no POW is lost.
- Rolling **exactly the current POW** still produces the effect, but the character's **soul is taken into the god's service** (the body dies).
- The POW lost follows the **Divine Intervention Table** (`13-quick-reference.md`): 01-05 → 0, rising to 91-95 → 10, and 96-00 → no effect / no loss. (The ch. VII example states it more simply as losing POW equal to the roll.)

## Elementals

Cults of the elemental gods grant spells to **summon and command elementals** (of fire, earth, air, water, darkness, etc.). An elemental's size and power scale with the points of the summoning spell; it serves until dismissed or destroyed. (Full elemental stats are setting/GM material — to summarise from p64 at the gate.)

## Cults

A cult write-up gives its god and Rune, who may join, the skills and weapons it teaches, its common battle magic, its specialty rune spells, its holy days, and the duties and restrictions of membership. The rulebook details example cults — **Orlanth** (storm), **Kyger Litor** (trolls/darkness), and the **Black Fang Brotherhood** (assassins) — as models; these are Gloranthan setting material and are not reproduced here.
