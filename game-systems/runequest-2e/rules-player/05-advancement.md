# Advancement

<!-- Source: rulebook ch.II p11-12 (characteristic training), ch.IV p24 (weapon training), ch.VI / appendix D p126 (skill training); experience in 02. Status: extracted-draft. -->

Abilities and some characteristics improve two ways: **experience** (free, from successful use) and **training** (paid, between adventures). Money is in **Lunars (L)**.

## Experience

After an ability is used successfully in earnest during an adventure, make an **experience check** at the adventure's end — see `02-core-resolution.md` (target = 100 − current ability, adjusted ±3% per INT point above 12 / below 9; success = +5%). POW rises only through magical contest (`02`/`08`).

## Training: the Cost Basis

Training is taught at a base of **100 L per 5% increase**, representing a week (six days) of 2 hours a day. No more than 8 hours a day may be trained, and training must be **continuous** — any break forfeits the money already spent. Costs rise with ability, and **training stops at 75%**; further gains must come through experience.

## Weapon Training

| Cut-and-thrust, 1H | Sickle | 15% | 200 | 400 | 800 |

## Skill Training

Non-combat skills (`04-skills.md`) train on the same banded basis. Costs per 5% increase, by current ability, are listed with each skill in the association's table; e.g. Climbing (Thieves) runs 200 / 400 / 800 / 1600 across the 05-25 / 30-50 / 55-75 / 80-100 bands, Lock Picking 500 / 1000 / 2000 / 4000, Tracking (Foresters) 200 / 400 / 800 / EXP. An "EXP." entry means that band is reached only by experience, not training.

## Characteristic Training

Once grown (about age 16), physical characteristics improve only by hard training:

| Characteristic | Cost | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| STR | 1000 L / point | Up to the highest of STR/CON/SIZ (the one already highest can't be trained) |
| CON | 2000 L / point | Up to the highest of STR/SIZ (likewise) |
| DEX | 1000 L / point | Up to the species maximum (21 for humans) |

INT and SIZ cannot be trained; POW rises only through magical success (`02`). Characteristic training uses the same 100 L / 2-hours-a-day basis, so the cost sets the weeks required (e.g. 1000 L = 10 weeks at 2 h/day).

## Associations: Guilds and Brotherhoods

Skills and weapons are taught by **associations** — fighting guilds and brotherhoods (STR/DEX/weapons), **Sages** (knowledge, languages, map making, oratory, evaluate treasure), **Thieves** (climbing, stealth, locks, traps, perception), **Armourers** (armour/weapon/shield making), **Mariners** (swimming), **Foresters** (tracking), **Horsemasters** (riding, warhorse training), and **Alchemists** (potions, `04-skills.md`). Thieves and players' guilds may extract service in place of, or alongside, money. Cult membership teaches spells (`08-magic.md`, `10-rune-magic-and-cults.md`).
