Glorantha holds many intelligent races besides humanity; many can be played as adventurers. This file gives the **stat-block format**, the **non-humanoid hit-location** chart, and a representative sample of creatures.

## Reading a Monster Stat Block

Characteristics are given as dice (with an average); humanoid characteristics roll like a human's (`3D6`) unless noted, and other body plans use larger dice. A block also gives **Move**, **average Hit Points**, the creature's **Treasure factor** and natural **Defense**, the **weapons** (SR, attack %, damage), **armour**, **spells**, and any special skills.

- A characteristic die addition of **+12, +18, or +24** counts as **one extra die** for the purpose of "each +X" effects.
- A creature's **CHA** applies among **its own race**; dealing with other races (e.g. joining another species' cult) reduces effective CHA by **10** unless the cult says otherwise.
- Damage bonus, hit points per location, strike rank, and the success bands work exactly as for adventurers (`01`, `02`, `06`, `07`).

## Non-Humanoid Hit Locations

Roll `D20` against the body plan. Representative charts:

**Four-legged mount / large quadruped**

| D20 | Location | | D20 | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01-02 | Right hind leg | | 11-13 | Right foreleg |
| 03-04 | Left hind leg | | 14-16 | Left foreleg |
| 05-07 | Hindquarters | | 17-18 | (continued) |
| 08-10 | Forequarters | | 19-20 | Head |

**Winged / flyer** (e.g. bird, dragonewt flyer): right/left hind leg, hindquarters, forequarters, **right wing**, **left wing**, right/left foreleg, head.

**Snake:** `01-06` tail, `07-14` body, `15-20` head. **Many-tentacled** (gargoyle, walktapus): each tentacle numbered, plus body/head.

## Sample Creatures

### Bison
A surly war-mount; thick-skinned; fights by charging.

`STR 3D6+24 (34-35) · CON 3D6 (10-11) · SIZ 3D6+24 (34-35) · POW 3D6 (10-11) · DEX 2D6 (7)` — **HP ~16-17, Move 12**.
- Butt (SR 8, 50%, `2D10+3D6`); Trample (SR 8, 50%, `6D6` vs a downed foe). Cannot butt and trample in the same round. **Armour 3** (skin).

### Bolo Lizard
A two-legged dinosaur mount that avoids direct combat.

`STR 2D6+12 (19) · CON 3D6 (10-11) · SIZ 2D6+12 (19) · POW 3D6 (10-11) · DEX 1D6+12 (15-16)` — **HP ~12-13, Move 12**.
- Bite (SR 7, 25%, `1D6+1D6`) and Kick (SR 6, 25%, `1D8+1D6`) — both in the same round. **Armour 1** (skin).

### Baboon
Intelligent, cursed plains/hill animals living in family groups of 15–20; use sling and spear plus claws and teeth, can wear bought armour, use all battle magic, and worship a monkey god (their rune magic is beast-slanted). **Playable.**

`STR 3D6+6 (16-17) · CON 3D6 (10-11) · SIZ 3D6 (10-11) · INT 3D6 (10-11) · POW 2D6+6 (13) · DEX 3D6` — **HP ~10-11, Move 10, Defense 05%, Treasure factor 8**. Fights with claws, teeth, spear, and sling.

### Broo (goatkin)
Human-bodied, goat-headed, chaos-tainted raiders (Rune of Chaos); often disease-carrying, with a fire-hardened spear. Playable as antagonists; many have a chaotic feature.

The chapter details many races, several playable: **humans**, **elves** (forest-bound, the Aldryami), **dwarves** (the Mostali), **trolls** (Uz, Darkness Rune) and **trollkin**, **ducks** (Durulz), **baboons**, **centaurs**, **minotaurs**, **dragonewts** (reincarnating, their own Rune), **broos**, **newtlings**, **morokanth**, and more — alongside beasts, dinosaurs, elementals, and chaos horrors.
