# Damage and Healing

<!-- Source: rulebook ch.III p17-18 (hit points per location, damage results, healing); appendix A p123. Status: extracted-draft. -->

## Hit Points: Total and by Location

A character has a **total Hit Points** score (CON ± SIZ/POW, `01-characteristics.md`) and a separate pool of **Hit Points per location**. A blow's damage (after armour) is subtracted from **both** the struck location and the total.

- **Total** Hit Points govern life: at **2 or 1** total the character is unconscious; at **0 or below** the character is dead.
- **Location** Hit Points govern wounds: dropping a location to **0 or below** triggers the effects below.

Hit points per location depend on total Hit Points:

| Location | 01-06 | 07-09 | 10-12 | 13-15 | 16-18 | 19-21 | each +3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Each Leg | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | +1 |
| Abdomen | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | +1 |
| Chest | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | +1 |
| Each Arm | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | +1 |
| Head | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | +1 |

## Effects of Location Damage

**A location reduced to 0 or below:**

| Location | Effect |
|---|---|
| Leg | Useless; the character falls (may fight from the ground in later rounds). |
| Abdomen | Both legs useless; must fall. Bleeds to death in **2 full turns** unless healed; may only heal self. |
| Chest | Falls, coughing blood, can do nothing (not even self-heal); bleeds to death in **2 full turns**. |
| Arm | Useless; anything held in it is dropped. Fights on with remaining limbs. |
| Head | Unconscious; heal within **2 full turns** or die. |

**A limb hit for more than twice its HP in one blow:** only twice the location's HP is taken off **total** Hit Points (the excess is ignored — ancient weapons lack hydrostatic shock), but the character is **functionally incapacitated** (in shock, cannot fight until healed; may heal self).

**A location hit for 6+ more than its HP in one blow:** a limb is **severed/maimed** (only a 6-point Healing spell or potion within 2 full turns reknits it); the **head, chest, or abdomen** is **instant death**. (This applies even if the excess would otherwise have been ignored by the rule above.)

## Healing

- **Healing spell** — restores one chosen location. A **2-point** Healing stops bleeding; a **6-point** Healing restores a severed/maimed limb (`08-magic.md`).
- **Healing potion** — a salve treats a specific location; an internally taken potion goes randomly among damaged areas, healing until its points run out.
- **Natural healing** — without magic, a character recovers **1 Hit Point per game week in each wounded location**.

A character at 0 or below **total** HP is dead regardless of location healing; bring total HP back above 0 (and stop any bleeding location) to stabilise.
