# Battle Magic Spells

<!-- Source: rulebook ch.V p32 (spell list & costs), p34-40 (spell descriptions). Status: extracted-draft. A few spells near the end of the list (Speedart, Strength, Vigor, Spirit Binding/Screen, Slow, Second Sight) need their descriptions confirmed at the gate. -->

Casting mechanics (focus, strike rank, POW-vs-POW resistance, duration types) are in `08-magic.md`. **Points** = minimum POW to cast (variable spells can be learned to higher levels); **Cost** is the price in Lunars to learn it; range is in metres. Most spells are **passive** (need not be maintained once cast); the only **active** spells — needing concentration — are **Fireblade** and **Invisibility**.

| Spell | Pts | Cost (L) | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Befuddle | 1 | 1500 | 80 | Target won't attack/cast/raise alarm; parries & defends at full; confusion ends next round. |
| Binding | 1 | 1500 | 80 | Halves the target's movement class (min 1). |
| Bladesharp | var* | var | 80 | Thrust/cut/hack weapon: +5% hit and +1 damage per point; cap +20%/+4. |
| Bludgeon | var* | var | 80 | As Bladesharp but for smashing weapons; cap +20%/+4. |
| Coordination | 2 | 1500 | 80 | +3 effective DEX (−1 SR, better DEX rolls); never above species max. |
| Countermagic | var* | var | 80 | Stops an incoming spell: eliminates one ≥2 pts weaker; mutual cancel if within 1 pt; a spell ≥2 pts stronger breaks through. Half effect vs rune magic; can't dispel spirits/elementals. |
| Darkwall | 2 | 1500 | 80 | 10×3 m, 6 cm wall of darkness, opaque both ways (even to dark-sight); movable 3 m/round. |
| Demoralize | 1 | 1500 | 80 | Target attacks at half, no offensive magic; defends at full. Cancelled by Fanaticism. |
| Detect Detection | 1 | 300 | 80 | Tells if a person/object is being detected by magic. |
| Detect Enemies | 1 | — | 40 | Direction & distance of anyone intending harm to a chosen person. (Instant.) |
| Detect Gems | 2 | 1000 | 40 | Number & position of gems; they glow. |
| Detect Gold | 1 | 300 | 40 | Direction, distance, amount of gold (to 0.1 kg). |
| Detect Life | 1 | 300 | 40 | Human-or-larger life (SIZ 3+), by direction/distance; splits human-size vs SIZ 22+. |
| Detect Magic | 1 | 300 | 40 | All magic objects/enchantments (they glow); not what or how strong. |
| Detect Silver | 1 | 200 | 40 | As Detect Gold, for silver. |
| Detect Spirit | 1 | 300 | 40 | Spirits, split POW ≤18 vs ≥19. |
| Detect Traps | 2 | 300 | 40 | Intent-to-trap (ambush, magical/physical trap), 3 m glow; not natural pits/poison. |
| Detect Undead | 1 | — | 40 | Undead (zombies, mummies, vampires), by size. |
| Detection Blank | var* | var | 80 | Blocks detection spells on caster/another without warning the detector. |
| Dispel Magic | var* | var | 80 | Dispels an active/passive spell of equal or fewer points (half effect vs rune magic). |
| Disruption | 1 | 500 | 80 | Overcome POW → `1D3` damage to a random location, **ignoring armour**. (Permanent.) |
| Dullblade | var* | var | 80 | Reverse of Bladesharp (−hit/−damage on a weapon). |
| Extinguish | 2 | 1000 | 40 | Puts out one large fire, or all small fires within 10 m; they stay out. (Permanent.) |
| Fanaticism | var* | var | 80 | +½ hit chance, but no parry/protective magic; Defense bonus at half. One target per point. Cancelled by Demoralize. |
| Farsee | 1 | 500 | touch | 10× telescopic vision, switchable. (Lasts 4 hours — the only such spell.) |
| Firearrow | 2 | 2000 | 80 | A fire arrow doing `3D6` (armour absorbs; not resistible — it's heat); one shot, burns up. |
| Fireblade | 4 | 2000 | 80 | Edged weapon flames for `3D6` (this replaces normal damage; armour absorbs; not resistible). **Active** — ends if caster is hurt. |
| Glamour | 2 | 2000 | 80 | +½ CHA for 2 minutes (crisis/ransom use, not for training). |
| Glue | var* | var | 80 | A 10×10 cm glue patch per point (or trade size for strength = POW × points); breaks on STR vs spell strength. Not on living flesh; objects must be at rest. |
| Harmonize | 2 | 1500 | 80 | One humanoid mimics the caster's actions; a second target releases the first. |
| Healing | var | var | touch | Heals the touched location; **2 pts** cauterise/stop bleeding, **6 pts** restore a severed limb (parts present). One location per 5 rounds. Not resurrection. (Permanent.) |
| Ignite | 1 | — | 40 | Lights anything normally burnable (like a lighter); skin/hair need overcoming POW. |
| Invisibility | 3 | — | 80 | Misdirects attention; ends if the subject attacks or is attacked. **Active.** |
| Ironhand | var* | var | 80 | As Bladesharp, for unarmed combat (whole body); cap +20%/+4. |
| Light | 1 | — | 80 | Lights a 12 m radius around a chosen object (read by it); immune to Extinguish. |
| Lightwall | 4 | — | 80 | As Darkwall but see-through from the caster's side; lights 15 m each side. |
| Mindspeech | var* | — | 160 | Verbal telepathy; +1 entity per point (links are not transitive; no spells/POW sent). |
| Mobility | 1 | — | 80 | Doubles the recipient's movement class (reverse of Binding). |
| Multimissile | var* | — | 80 | +1 missile per point (cap 4 pts/5 missiles); copies can't crit; each rolled separately. |
| Protection | var* | — | 80 | +1 armour to all locations per point (cap 4); criticals still ignore it. |
| Repair | 2 | — | touch | Restores a broken object (all pieces present); does not restore lost enchantment. |
| Shimmer | var* | — | 80 | +5% Defense per point (cap +20%). |
| Silence | 1 | — | 80 | Suppresses a subject's movement noise; doesn't block spellcasting. |
| Speedart | 1 | — | 80 | One missile gets +15% to hit and +3 damage (any missile, incl. thrown/bullets). Incompatible with Firearrow, Multimissile. |
| Spirit Binding | 1 | — | 80 | Binds a spirit into a familiar (a cult-bred animal) or a magic object; freed if the binder dies. (Permanent.) |
| Spirit Shield (Screen) | var* | — | 80 | In spirit combat, each point destroys 2 POW of the attacking spirit; must be cast **before** engaging. Incompatible with Countermagic, Protection, Shimmer. |
| Strength | 2 | — | 80 | +½ STR (round up), never above species max. Incompatible with Vigor. |
| Vigor | 4 | — | 80 | +½ CON (round up), never above species max. Incompatible with Strength. |
| Xenohealing | var | — | touch | As Healing, but heals non-human creatures that ordinary Healing won't (confirm details at gate). |

\* **Variable** spell: can be learned/enchanted to higher point levels up to its cap.
