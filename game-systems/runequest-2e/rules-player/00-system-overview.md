# System Overview

<!-- Source: rulebook ch.I p1-7 (introduction); structure/mechanics overview. Status: extracted-draft. -->

RuneQuest is a fantasy role-playing game and an ancestor of Basic Role-Playing. Players run **adventurers** through scenarios set by a **referee**; the adventurer uses combat, magic, and other skills to win treasure and survive the referee's monsters and traps. The default world is **Glorantha**, but the rules stand on their own. Survivors grow by improving skills, raising characteristics, gaining POW, and joining cults.

## Dice

Percentile (`D100`) drives skill and attack resolution; `D4`/`D6`/`D8`/`D10`/`D20` are used for damage, characteristics, and tables. `D100` is rolled as two `D10`/`D20` for tens and ones (`00` = 100).

## Core Mechanic

Almost everything an adventurer attempts is an ability rated as a **percentage**. Roll `D100`:

- **Roll ≤ the ability** → success. A roll of one-fifth or less (rounding) is a **critical**; a high roll (96–00, by skill) is a **fumble**. Full success bands are in `02-core-resolution.md`.
- Contests of raw characteristic against characteristic (STR vs STR, magic point attack vs POW, poison potency vs CON) use the **Resistance Table** (`02-core-resolution.md`).
- Abilities improve by an **experience check** after they are used successfully in earnest, or by paid **training** (`05-advancement.md`).

## Characteristics → Categories

Seven characteristics (STR, CON, SIZ, INT, POW, DEX, CHA; `3D6` each for humans) set everything else. High or low characteristics give **category modifiers** — percentage bonuses to whole families of ability (Attack, Parry, Defense, Perception, Stealth, Manipulation, Knowledge) — and derive **Hit Points**, the **damage bonus**, and **magic points** (= POW). See `01-characteristics.md`.

## The Melee Round at a Glance

A melee round is ~12 seconds and resolves in **strike-rank (SR)** order — lower SR acts first. An adventurer's SR comes from DEX, SIZ, and weapon length (spells from POW spent). Attacks are made against a **hit location** (`06-combat.md`); damage is tracked per location and against total Hit Points (`07-damage-and-healing.md`). A defender may **parry** (with a weapon/shield, which itself takes damage) or rely on **Defense** (subtracted from the attacker's chance to hit).

## Two Kinds of Magic

- **Battle magic** (`08-magic.md`, `09-battle-magic-spells.md`) — common spells powered by magic points, learned from cults, temples, and teachers.
- **Rune magic** (`10-rune-magic-and-cults.md`) — powerful, mostly one-use cult spells granted to rune priests and rune lords by the gods, tied to cult membership.

Spirits, shamans, and divine intervention extend both systems.
