# GM Source

GM-only case material — never shown to players. Replace this guidance with your
own:

- What is really going on: the solution, the culprit, the motive.
- Secrets and twists, and when each is revealed.
- Each key NPC's true agenda and what they know (and conceal).
- The clue chain that leads to the answer, and where each clue is found.
- Pacing notes and contingencies if the players stall or go off-script.
