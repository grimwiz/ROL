# Player Source

Player-visible case material — everything here can be read by the players and
feeds the player-facing summaries. Replace this guidance with your own:

- The opening hook: how the case reaches the investigators.
- What the players are briefed on, and the facts they can find out openly.
- The public people, places, and events relevant to the case.

Keep secrets, the solution, twists, and NPCs' true motives in the GM Source
(`GM/gm.md`) — this file is shown to players.
