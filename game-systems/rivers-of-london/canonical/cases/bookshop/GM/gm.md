# The Bookshop - GM Notes

This is a compact, paraphrased run sheet for the built-in Bookshop test case. It is GM-only. The player-facing start state is intentionally short in `input/player.md`, so generated player summaries begin with a clean initial brief.

## Case Purpose

Use this case to test:

- Player-safe source material in `input/`.
- GM-only secrets and pacing notes in `GM/`.
- Handouts as separate player-visible Markdown files.
- Player and GM map/artifact visibility.
- AI Support questions about scenario facts, clues, NPCs, and likely next steps.

The case is designed for one to four new Folly investigators and can run in about two hours.

## Core Mystery

Stonewaters Bookshop is experiencing what looks like poltergeist activity. The apparent haunting is actually a newly formed **genius loci of books and reading**.

The hidden cause:

- The building once housed a famous cockfighting venue.
- Violent emotional vestigia soaked into the building, especially around the central dome and basement.
- For years, the basement children's section and Saturday Story Time gave that latent power a new emotional shape.
- The children's section was recently moved upstairs.
- The young genius loci feels abandoned and is acting out with books, toys, and escalating tantrums.

The solution is not exorcism or force. The investigators need to recognise the spirit's loneliness and arrange regular stories, audiobooks, or another credible source of attention.

## Important Spoiler Boundary

Players should not know at the start that the culprit is a genius loci. They may initially think in terms of a poltergeist, a spell, an intruder, or a prank. Let the evidence narrow that down.

## Investigator Setup

All investigators should be new Folly recruits. At least one investigator should ideally have the **Magical** advantage and **Magic** skill.

Useful occupations:

- Police Officer/Detective
- Architect
- Author
- Chancer
- Lecturer
- Librarian
- Parapsychologist

Useful skills and spells:

- Art/Craft (Architecture), History, Occult
- Computer Use and Tech
- Observation, Research, Read Person, Social
- Sense Vestigia and Magic
- Werelight

If the group lacks a needed expert skill, use Trying Your Luck rather than blocking progress.

## Timeline

- Saturday, late evening: Warwick Anderson is working alone in the basement.
- About 00:30: books attack Warwick. He calls 999.
- Sunday, 08:00: Nightingale briefs the investigators.
- Before 11:00: investigators interview Warwick and inspect Stonewaters.
- Late morning: investigators report to Nightingale.
- Afternoon: research and planning at the Folly.
- 20:00: return to Stonewaters after closing.
- 23:00-00:30: the genius loci manifests.
- After manifestation: identify the cause and negotiate a peaceful solution.

## Scene Flow

### 1. Morning Briefing

Nightingale sends the investigators to New Row. They should interview Warwick Anderson at the Cappuccino Lounge, then inspect Stonewaters with Saffron Jackson.

Nightingale's briefing should convey:

- Warwick was injured while rearranging stock in the basement.
- Police found no break-in, theft, or obvious mundane attacker.
- There were clear signs of disturbance.
- The Folly needs an Initial Vestigium Assessment before ordinary police treat it as mundane.
- The shop wants to reopen at 11:00.

Use Nightingale as a gentle tutorial tool. He can ask them to report back after the first investigation so you can check what they found and correct misunderstandings.

### 2. Cappuccino Lounge

Warwick is frightened, tired, and guarded by PC Karnam Singh. He relaxes when the investigators show warrant cards or Folly consultant IDs.

Give or reveal `input/handout-warwick-statement.md`.

Follow-up clues:

- He was moving celebrity chef/cookery books.
- The art and children's sections swapped places last week.
- Saturday Story Time moved upstairs because prams and stairs were a problem.
- The building is old, odd-shaped, and difficult to monitor.
- Warwick calls it a poltergeist.

Roll prompts:

- Magic or Occult: ghosts normally do not throw things around to hurt people; a violent "poltergeist" may be something more serious.
- Hard Magic/Occult: a true poltergeist needs a long time to form, so the building's age matters.

### 3. Stonewaters Exterior

The front door has plywood over the panel broken by the response team.

Roll prompts:

- Art/Craft (Architecture) or History: the frontage looks Georgian and probably listed.
- Read Person on Saffron: she is relieved by Folly help but irritated by the disruption.
- Observation inside: the investigators may notice the central dome and, on a harder result, the convex mirror/security blind spot.

### 4. Saffron Jackson

Saffron wants the shop safe and open. She is professional, worried about stock damage, and worried about reputation.

Give or reveal `input/handout-saffron-statement.md`.

Follow-up clues:

- She confirms the children's and art sections swapped recently.
- Saturday Story Time is easier upstairs.
- She knows nothing useful about the dome.
- She knows no ghost stories about the building.
- She can show the CCTV if persuaded.
- She can arrange a night return if convinced.

Roll prompts:

- Social to view CCTV or arrange after-hours access.
- Police warrant cards should grant a bonus die where authority helps.
- Art/Craft (Architecture) or History about the dome: it may be a surviving part of an older hall-like interior.

### 5. Initial Vestigium Assessment

Books dampen vestigia, so the signal is weak except beneath the dome and in the basement.

IVA results:

- Regular Sense Vestigia: slaughterhouse and rotting straw.
- Hard Sense Vestigia: the smells plus shouting, excitement, desire, and disappointment.
- Failure: only ordinary central London background noise.
- Fumble: the building feels strangely blank.

Research or History can connect the slaughterhouse/cockfighting impression to the site's past. Reveal `input/handout-research-note.md` when appropriate.

### 6. Basement Search

Key features:

- Stairs descend from the ground floor.
- A till point and phone sit near the cookery section.
- Art books were the likely source of the impact.
- A security camera covers the dead spot near the till.
- The staff corridor leads to storeroom, staffroom, and Saffron's office.

Clue logic:

- A human attacker would have had to get behind Warwick, throw many heavy books at once, hide after the attack, and leave no sign.
- A spell powerful enough to move that many books would probably have damaged the electronics.
- If the till and CCTV still work, a normal Newtonian spell is less likely.

Spell handling:

- Ordinary spell success works normally.
- Ordinary spell failure behaves weakly or oddly.
- A fumble or failed pushed spell sands the till, CCTV, and powered mobile devices in the basement.
- Werelight changes colour and is consumed, suggesting something is feeding on magic but is not a normal ghost.
- Boosted Werelight or multiple Werelights provoke a stronger reaction.

### 7. CCTV

The recording shows Warwick near the edge of the frame. Books suddenly enter the shot in mid-flight, knock him down, and he staggers toward the till. The footage does not reveal a visible attacker.

Magic-informed interpretation:

- The working camera argues against a large Newtonian spell.
- The event looks more like a localised manifestation than a human assault.

### 8. Case Update At The Folly

Have the investigators report to Nightingale over tea in the atrium.

By this point they should have some of:

- The attack came from the art section.
- A mundane attacker is implausible.
- A spell is unlikely if electronics survived.
- The building has cockfighting vestigia.
- The art and children's sections recently swapped.
- Werelight, if used, suggests a non-ghost magical presence.

If they are lost, Nightingale nudges them toward research, hypothesis, and a night return.

### 9. Research

Mundane library or internet:

- Regular Research: listed Georgian bookshop, basic opening information.
- Hard Research: the site is connected to an eighteenth-century cockfighting venue and the dome is likely significant.
- Library research can reveal the same information, taking longer on weaker results.

Magical library:

- Ghosts do not usually try to injure people.
- True poltergeists take a long time to form.
- This may not be a ghost.

If they did not cast Werelight and need a lead, Magic or Occult can suggest using a ghost-hunting dog. Toby is unavailable; Mr Fitzroy can lend Ernie.

### 10. Return To Stonewaters

Saffron meets them after closing at 20:00, explains the alarm and locks, and leaves. If Ernie is present, she is suspicious but allows him in if reassured.

Ask where they set up:

- Basement: quiet until someone leaves, casts magic, or Ernie needs a break.
- Manager's office: they can watch CCTV.
- Staffroom: tea, coffee, chairs, and possible camera setup.

Search before settling:

- Observation success confirms no one else is in the shop.

### 11. Manifestations

If watched remotely, books float and arrange themselves into four piles at about 23:30. Occult or History can recall a similar book-stacking case at the British Library.

If everyone leaves the basement:

- Observation hears rustling, then books moving.
- Sense Vestigia detects blood, plasticine, crossed legs, and chicken feathers.
- Hard Sense Vestigia also identifies a powerful non-ghost presence.

If Ernie is present:

- Ernie barks or yaps at the critical moment.
- His aggression startles the young genius loci and may trigger the book barrage.

If the party ignores the first stacks:

- Another stack appears around midnight.
- If ignored until about 00:30, the spirit escalates and empties the shelves.

### 12. Book Barrage

When the basement erupts, everyone present rolls Athletics or DEX. A prior successful Observation may grant a bonus die.

Results:

- Success: dive behind cover, no damage.
- Failure: make a Luck roll.
- Luck success: 1 damage from lighter books.
- Luck failure: 2 damage from hardbacks; Bloodied and impaired if no previous damage.
- Luck fumble: 3 damage and briefly knocked out; Down and impaired if no previous damage.
- Natural Toughness or a Met stab vest reduces this book damage by 1.
- Ernie automatically gets to cover.

After the incident:

- Hurt and Bloodied recover as normal after the fight.
- Down leaves the investigator Hurt and impaired until the end of the next day unless Luck is spent under the normal rules.

### 13. Realisation

Ground-floor inspection shows children's books scattered toward the basement stairwell while other sections are untouched.

INT prompt:

- Regular success: the old cockfighting vestigia has transformed into a genius loci, and moving the children's section triggered the behaviour.
- Hard success: the spirit behaves like a young abandoned child and is confined to the basement.
- Failure: the move of the children's section is obviously important, but the nature of the entity remains unclear.
- If no one gets there, Nightingale can suggest thinking about demi-monde beings powerful enough to do this.

### 14. Resolution

The genius loci needs reliable attention and stories.

Viable solutions:

- Weekly in-person bedtime story, preferably on Saturdays.
- Audiobooks played in the basement twice weekly and when staff are working late.
- Communicate with letter flashcards from the children's section.
- Ask an appropriate genius loci or lower fae to help communicate.
- Trade a story for the spirit tidying up the basement.

Quid pro quo:

- The spirit can restack books in exchange for a long story.
- Reader makes a Luck roll.
- Success: the story satisfies it for a week.
- Failure: it wants the story repeated or extended.
- Fumble: it rejects the story and starts throwing things again.

Saffron accepts any practical plan that prevents further injuries. Warwick leaves Stonewaters for another bookshop and will not return.

## Rewards

Run an Investigator Development Phase.

- Standard award: 1 development point per session.
- If there will be downtime before the next case, optionally award 1 additional development point for training.
- Employment and affluence normally do not change unless the investigators caused major avoidable damage.
