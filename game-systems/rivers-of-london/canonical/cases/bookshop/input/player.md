# The Bookshop - Player Brief

You are new Folly investigators assigned to a small Falcon inquiry in Covent Garden.

At 8:00 on Sunday morning, DCI Nightingale briefs you at the Folly. A bookseller named **Warwick Anderson** was injured at **Stonewaters Bookshop** on New Row at about 00:30 while working in the basement. Responding officers found no sign of forced entry beyond their own arrival, no obvious theft, and no mundane suspect, but there was clear evidence of a disturbance in the basement.

Your initial assignment:

- Interview Warwick Anderson at the **Cappuccino Lounge** on New Row.
- Meet **Saffron Jackson**, the manager of Stonewaters Bookshop.
- Inspect the shop, including the basement.
- Carry out an **Initial Vestigium Assessment**.
- Report your findings back to DCI Nightingale.

The shop hopes to open at 11:00, so the first phase is under time pressure.

Useful investigator capabilities for this case include **Magic**, **Sense Vestigia**, **Observation**, **Research**, **History**, **Occult**, **Art/Craft (Architecture)**, **Social**, **Tech**, and **Computer Use**. **Werelight** may be especially relevant if the incident is ghost-related.
