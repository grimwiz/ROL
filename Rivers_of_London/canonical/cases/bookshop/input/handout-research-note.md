# Handout - Stonewaters Research Note

Stonewaters Bookshop occupies an unusually shaped listed building near Covent Garden. Its visible Georgian fabric suggests several older premises were combined into one shop.

Research can uncover that the site was once associated with an eighteenth-century cockfighting venue. The most important surviving architectural feature is the dome above the shop's central till area. That older use may explain the unusual vestigia detected in the building.

The building's current layout matters:

- Children's books recently moved from the basement to the ground floor.
- Art books moved into the old children's area.
- Cookery stock was being rearranged when Warwick was attacked.
- The basement still seems to be the focus of the disturbance.
