- Role: keeps Warwick company at the Cappuccino Lounge.

## The Spirit of Books and Reading

Newly formed genius loci and hidden cause of the disturbance.

- Role: antagonist/problem, but not malicious.
- Wants: stories, attention, and the return of the emotional stimulus it lost when the children's section moved.
- Limits: non-corporeal, immature, non-verbal, no Glamour.
- Behaviour: stacks books, throws books, drags children's books toward the basement, feeds on Werelight, and lashes out when ignored or startled.
