# The Bookshop - Clue Map

## Essential Clues

| Clue | Where It Appears | What It Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warwick was alone and the doors were locked. | Warwick interview, police briefing. | Mundane intruder is unlikely. |
| Art books attacked Warwick from the shelves. | Warwick statement, basement search, CCTV. | The origin point is the art section, not cookery. |
| Art and children's sections swapped recently. | Warwick, Saffron, player handouts. | The move is the triggering event. |

| CCTV still works. | Saffron's office. | Heavy Newtonian spellcasting is unlikely. |
| Books dampen vestigia. | Any IVA explanation. | Explains weak signals except at focus points. |
| Children's books trail toward basement. | After the book barrage. | The spirit wants the old story-time attention back downstairs. |

## Red Herrings And False Starts

- "Poltergeist" is Warwick's best guess, not the truth.
- A human attacker is possible only until the investigators think through hiding, locked doors, and the volume of books.
- A Newtonian spell is possible only until the working electronics and Werelight behaviour are considered.
- Ernie may look like a comic distraction, but he is a valid way to push the manifestation into the open.

## If The Players Stall

Use Nightingale to ask questions rather than solve it:

- What changed in the bookshop recently?
- What did the vestigia feel like?
- What did Werelight do?
- Why did the electronics survive?
- What beings besides ghosts can become attached to places?
- What would a lonely child do if everyone stopped visiting?

## End-State Tests

The players have solved the case when they can explain:

- The entity is a newly formed genius loci.
- It is linked to books, reading, the basement, and the old cockfighting residue.
- Moving the children's section removed its attention source.
- It needs regular story attention, not destruction.
