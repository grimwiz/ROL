# Case Seeds

Short, self-contained Falcon hooks a GM can drop into play or expand into a full
case. Each gives a situation and an open question; the answer is the GM's to set.
Cross-references point at `scenario/folly-and-london.md`.

## A Pet Project
Someone restores a long-derelict former **County Practitioner's** house and is
woken nightly by tapping at the window - a huge rook, soon joined by more in
growing numbers. Ordinary corvid behaviour, or did the dead practitioner bind a
spirit in bird form as a familiar, contracted to serve the master of the house
until dismissed - and is it now an angry supernatural threat?

## It's In The Trees
A tenant farmer on the Folly-owned **Casterbrook (Ambrose) estate** reports
something killing his cattle, coming from the woods at night; he's tried hunting
it but "it don't seem natural." Did a Casterbrook pupil or teacher leave something
monstrous growing in the grounds, or is it just a wild beast?

## The Wise Woman
A woman complains that a sorceress she paid to win back her errant husband took
her money - and now the husband has vanished entirely. A fraudulent psychic, or a
**Society of the Rose** member who decided the kindest favour was to remove a
rotten husband for good?

## Walkies
The investigators are asked to exercise the semi-sentient **Book of Cunning
Device** around the Folly (it's not allowed outside). In any room with other
books it skitters up the shelves dumping tomes on their heads - until it finds
what it sought: an old volume visibly infested with bookworms. The Book was trying
to warn of a threat to its less communicative siblings. Are the bookworms more
than mundane larvae?

## The Lost Mummy
Tourists report a mummy walking around the **Petrie Museum** (UCL); staff say none
are missing. Research shows Egyptologist-witch **Dr Margaret Murray** once warded
the cabinets against escape. A student prank - or the British Museum's
long-missing mummy paying a visit, and if so, with what plans?

## The Key
An esoteric bookshop reports a house-clearance box of old magical tomes; one book
has been hollowed out to hide a small key. Its vestigia reads as burnt tallow,
dried blood, and a music-box melody. What does the key unlock?

## Skygarden Souvenirs
A demi-monde lead says a small floating plastic object will be auctioned at an
upcoming **nazareth**; meanwhile, children trespassing on the **Skygarden** crater
claim to have found things floating in the air. Are the two connected - and is
anyone in danger?

## Blue Beads
A girl hospitalised since losing her blue-bead necklace in **Portobello Road**
market says the beads were a charm that helped her breathe. Most "breathing beads"
are mere folklore, but a trader saw go-between **Zachary Palmer** with a set on the
day they vanished; Zach says he found them and gave them to a **Quiet Person**
child with a serious breathing condition. What do the investigators do?

## Forging Connections
Overnight interference at an archaeological dig at Furnivall Gardens,
Hammersmith - a witness saw a huge, hugely muscled woman leaving late with a hammer
over her shoulder (find her at the riverside Dove pub). A possible **Sons of
Weyland** connection: who is she, and did she sabotage the dig or protect it?

## Magic Bus
A vintage double-decker - famously driven across the gap of an opening **Tower
Bridge** in `1952` - is reported stolen. Its owner insists it's magical, making it
a Falcon matter. Is it genuinely magical, or is this a publicity stunt for the
owner's new tour business?

## The Drowned Sailor
A drowned sailor on the Thames foreshore has pearls where his eyes should be and a
copy of T.S. Eliot's *The Waste Land* in his backpack. The Spring (King) Tide is
`48` hours away and storms are forecast. Is this a ritual to flood London - and
who is behind it?

## River Head
A metal detectorist is found decapitated by **Vale of Health Pond** on the
midwinter solstice, head missing. The work of the legendary "Hatchett Man," a
copy-cat, or a coincidental murder? MOLA's Camilla Turner notes the **Paternoster
Society** once asked about ancient Celtic head-taking rites done to win magical
power. Are they involved?
