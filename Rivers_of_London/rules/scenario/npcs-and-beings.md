The rulebook's Rogues' Gallery is a roster of ready-to-use **named NPCs** and
**example demi-monde beings**. This file is a GM-facing index — who's in it and
what they are, in one line each — not a reproduction of the write-ups.

Where to find the detail:

- **Full stat blocks** for the named cast are imported as NPC sheets in
  `../../globaldata/npcs/<slug>.json` (also surfaced in the app's character
  tools). Cited below as `npcs/<slug>`.
- **Being mechanics** (glamour, demi-monde Luck, thematic power, cold iron,
  genii locorum, ghosts, vampires, unicorns, Quiet People, non-Newtonian
  fighting) are in `../11-demi-monde.md`.
- **Setting lore** for the recurring figures and powers is in
  `../../globaldata/{key-npcs,demi-monde,rivers-and-genii-locorum}.md`.

## Notes

- The named cast above is the rulebook's Rogues' Gallery. **Case-specific NPCs**
  (e.g. the Bookshop's Warwick Anderson, Saffron Jackson, the Spirit of Books
  and Reading, Ernie) belong to their cases, not to this general roster.
- This index reproduces no profile prose. For a character's voice and standing
  knowledge, see their persona file under `../../globaldata/npcs/personas/`
  (where one exists); for mechanics, the sheet and `../11-demi-monde.md`.
