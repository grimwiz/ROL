# Spells

<!-- Source: newtonian-magic, printed pages 178-193, including spell list/tree images on pages 179, 181, and 185. Status: extracted-draft. -->

This file contains the Chapter 4 Newtonian spell catalog. General casting procedure, magic points, mastery, boosting limits, technology sanding, spell detection, and HTD are in `09-magic.md`.

## Spell Rules

- A spell's base magic point cost equals its order.
- Only mastered spells can be boosted.
- A spell can only be boosted if its entry includes a Boost rule.
- A prerequisite listed as mastered means the prerequisite spell must be mastered.
- A normal prerequisite only needs to be known.
- When a spell attack says it resolves like firearms, roll Magic instead of Firearms. The same roll determines casting and hit. Use the caster's DEX for the damage roll unless the spell says otherwise.
- Society of the Rose spells have no special rule by default; the tag records origin and style.

## First Order

### Aqua

Prerequisite: none.

Creates a ping-pong-ball-sized sphere of water just above the caster's palm.

Boost: each additional magic point either creates another small water sphere or enlarges the existing sphere.

### Flash Bulb

Prerequisite: none.

Creates a bright flash of light. When cast in front of a target, the target rolls POW.

| POW result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Success | No listed mechanical effect. |
| Failure | The target is stunned and partly blinded for `1D10` rounds, loses their next action, and has a penalty die on sight-dependent actions while stunned. |

### Impello

Prerequisite: none.

Pushes, pulls, or levitates an object. The caster cannot lift themself, even by lifting something they are attached to. It can push an opponent back 1 metre/yard, potentially opening a chance to flee.

Use POW where a comparable mundane action would use STR. Lifting an apple needs no roll; moving heavier objects may require POW, with difficulty based on the target object's effective STR. Precisely targeting a small object that could be hidden in one hand may require POW.

Against a character, the GM may call for opposed POW vs STR. Impello is not designed to damage, but if the movement causes an obvious harmful event, such as a fall, use the other-damage rules.

### Noisemaker

Prerequisite: Werelight.

Creates a small expanding gas cloud with a comic noise. If large enough, the cloud can also make objects shake. Its main use is distraction, including at a distance.

### Rock Drill

Prerequisite: Impello.

Repeatedly hammers a surface with force. It breaks either:

- 7.5 cm / 3 inches of rock or concrete per round.
- 30 cm / 1 foot of compacted earth per round.

Boost: on each following round, spend 1 additional magic point to sustain the drilling.

### Scindere

Prerequisite: none.

Holds an object stationary, up to about the size and weight of a large watermelon, roughly 10 kg / 22 lb. Moving an object held by Scindere requires opposed STR vs the caster's POW. Moving it magically, such as with Impello, requires opposed POW between casters.

Boost:

| Additional magic points | Total cost | Maximum object sense |
|---:|---:|---|
| +1 | 2 | A human being. |
| +2 | 3 | A horse or cow. |
| +3 | 4 | An average car, with larger benchmarks by GM judgement. |

Before the spell ends, 1 additional magic point can sustain it for another 5 minutes.

### Snapdragon

Prerequisite: none.

Society of the Rose spell. Produces a flash and loud crack suitable for distraction or intimidation.

### Terra

Prerequisite: none.

Creates a golf-ball-sized rock or a handful of earth. Common uses include making small stones or smothering a fire.

Boost: each additional magic point either creates another small amount of earth/stone or enlarges the existing amount.

### Werelight

Prerequisite: none.

Creates a golf-ball-sized ball of bright light, about as bright as a domestic lightbulb. The standard light gives off no heat. It moves with the caster's hand if held there, or it can be placed in a static position. Duration: 5 minutes.

Ghosts are attracted to the magical energy and may feed from it. While feeding, the light shifts from soft white or milky blue toward yellow, red, or dark crimson; the ghost becomes visible and may converse.

Boost:

- Spend 1 additional magic point to increase brightness or create an additional ball.
- With 1 boost point, the light is painful to look at and can illuminate a large hall.
- Before the spell ends, spend 1 additional magic point to sustain it for another 5 minutes.

Mastered: the caster can alter brightness, colour, and visible spectrum. Very low-power light can avoid damaging nearby microprocessors. Infrared use can create heat.

## Second Order

### Candano

Prerequisite: none.

Society of the Rose spell, also called Tanglefoot. Creates a magical bolo around a target's lower legs and ankles.

In combat, resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. If the spell hits, the target rolls DEX or STR, whichever is higher.

| Target roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hard success or better | Target avoids the bolo or breaks free. |
| Less than Hard success | Target's legs are entangled for 2 rounds. If they try to move, they fall prone. They cannot stand as a free action until the spell ends. Attacks against them while prone gain a bonus die. |

### Car Killer

Prerequisites: Werelight + Impello.

Uses magic's microprocessor-damaging side effect to disable a modern vehicle or other powered device containing a microprocessor. Apply the technology-sanding rules in `09-magic.md` where range, power state, or carried devices matter.

### Fireball

Prerequisites: Impello + Werelight or Flash Bulb.

Creates a loud, bright, relatively slow fireball projectile. Modern ballistic armour can stop it.

In combat, resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll damage with the caster's DEX. The basic spell adds 1 weapon damage to the damage roll.

Boost: each additional magic point adds +1 weapon damage. The effect can be described as one larger fireball or multiple fireballs; the mechanics are identical.

Mastered: the caster can alter fireball size. Narrow-gauge fireballs can make wounds resemble handgun injuries.

### Impello Palma

Prerequisite: Impello.

Creates an invisible hand-like force that swats objects or people. In combat, resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. A successful hit inflicts exactly 1 damage.

Boost: additional magic points can create a temporary barrier lasting while the caster concentrates, or 5 minutes, whichever ends first.

Barrier interaction:

- Running into the barrier fails and inflicts 1 damage, like striking plate glass.
- Slow deliberate pressure requires opposed POW vs the caster. On success, the person passes through unharmed. On failure, they cannot pass.
- The barrier does not protect against damage; use Shield for protection.

Barrier size:

| Additional magic points | Total cost | Barrier size |
|---:|---:|---|
| +1 | 3 | Typical single door. |
| +2 | 4 | Double that size, such as a double door. |

### Impello Vibrato

Prerequisite: Rock Drill.

Vibrates inert matter such as earth, brick, or concrete. It can knock loose out-of-reach objects, drop objects from a surface, or collapse/crumble structures. It has no visible effect until the caster ends the spell.

The caster must concentrate for 3 or 5 rounds and can take no other actions. If injured or distracted, the caster rolls POW; failure disrupts the spell.

For a structural collapse:

- With a successful INT roll made before casting to identify critical load-bearing points, the collapse takes 3 rounds and happens immediately after the caster's turn in the third round.
- Without that INT targeting, pushing over the weakened structure takes 5 rounds.

Boost: spend 2 additional magic points to upgrade the invisible hammer effect to a sledgehammer effect.

### Invisible Catch

Prerequisite: Impello.

Catches living or non-living objects in mid-air. If the target is moving quickly, such as a thrown object, apply one penalty die to the casting roll. Use POW where a comparable mundane catch would use STR. Catching an apple needs no POW roll; catching a heavier object, such as a falling child, may require POW.

The spell can catch magical effects such as fireballs. It cannot catch bullets.

Boost: to hold an incoming thrown weapon, heavy object, fireball, or similar projectile, the total magic points spent must equal or exceed the damage the projectile would have inflicted. Bullets remain too fast.

### Light Bulb

Prerequisites: Scindere + Werelight.

Fixes a werelight in a chosen location so that it resembles a normal light fitting. Duration: 5 minutes.

Boost:

- Each additional magic point increases either size or brightness.
- Before the spell ends, spend 1 additional magic point to sustain it for another 5 minutes.

If the caster wants to fix the light somewhere they cannot physically reach, combine the spell with Impello to manoeuvre it into place. That combined version is third order, has a base cost of 3 magic points, and uses the same boost options.

### Serra Obscura

Prerequisite: Shield.

Creates a small force blade up to 6 cm / 2.4 inches long, extending from the caster's palm, thumb, or finger. It can cut rope and similar materials or function as a melee weapon.

- Duration: 1 minute, or 6 combat rounds.
- Combat skill: Fighting.
- Base damage: 1.

### Shield

Prerequisite: none.

Creates an air shield in front of the caster. It protects against bullets, melee weapons, and unarmed attacks. Resolve each incoming attack normally, then reduce damage by the shield's value.

Basic shield:

- Reduces each attack's damage by 2.
- Works against each bullet separately.
- Can absorb 20 total damage before failing.
- Stacks with armour.

Boost: each additional magic point lets the caster choose one:

- Fix the shield in place.
- Increase the shield's damage reduction by 1. This also raises its total damage capacity by 10, so reduction 3 has capacity 30, and so on.

On each following round, 1 additional magic point can sustain the shield and add another 10 points to total damage capacity.

Mastered: the caster can angle the shield to deflect projectiles. This changes capacity from ten times the shield value to twenty times the shield value. An unboosted mastered shield therefore has capacity 40.

### Wandering Light

Prerequisites: Impello + Werelight.

Creates a werelight that keeps a fixed position relative to the caster: behind, beside, in front, overhead, or similar. Duration: 5 minutes.

Boost:

- Each additional magic point increases size or brightness.
- Before the spell ends, spend 1 additional magic point to sustain it for another 5 minutes.

### Waterbomb

Prerequisites: Aqua + Impello.

Creates a balloon-sized water ball where directed.

Boost: each additional magic point lets the caster choose one:

- Propel the water ball to hit a target.
- Chill the water.

The spell inflicts no damage, but can distract. A struck target rolls POW, with one penalty die if the water is chilled.

| POW result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Success | No listed mechanical penalty. |
| Failure | Target loses their next action and applies a penalty die to any roll made as that next action. |

## Third Order

### Ball of Lightning

Prerequisites: Impello + Shield.

Creates a fist-sized lightning ball projected at a target. Resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll damage with the caster's DEX. The basic spell adds 2 weapon damage.

Boost: each additional magic point adds +1 weapon damage.

### Bumblebee

Prerequisite: Wandering Light.

Creates a golf-ball-sized orange-red sphere that squeals and flies erratically for a few rounds. It may initially shoot the wrong way before returning and flitting toward the intended direction. It is a hard-to-ignore distraction and does not otherwise do much.

### Glitter Bomb

Prerequisite: Waterbomb.

Creates an icy projectile that freezes an area where it hits. A wave of cool air spreads from the impact point. Blast radius: 2 metres/yards.

Targets in the blast may suffer damage. Resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll damage with the caster's DEX. The basic spell adds 2 weapon damage.

Boost: each additional magic point increases the radius by 1 metre/yard.

### Grasping Hand

Prerequisite: Impello (mastered).

Moves or damages structures with superhuman force. The standard spell can rip a locked car door off its hinges, roll a standard car onto its roof, or rip locked doors out of their frames without needing STR.

Against living targets, the spell throws the target a number of metres/yards equal to the caster's POW. Use the other-damage rules. The standard spell inflicts Severe damage.

Boost:

| Additional magic points | Total cost | Object effect | Living-target damage |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| +1 | 4 | Roll a large vehicle, such as a heavy car or pickup. | Deadly |
| +2 | 5 | Roll a delivery truck. | Terminal |
| +3 | 6 | Roll an articulated lorry or rip a bank-vault door away. | Splat |

### Ice Blast

Prerequisite: Waterbomb.

Creates a conical blast of freezing ice particles from the caster's hand.

- Length: 30 metres/yards.
- Width: hand-width at the caster, 3 metres/yards at the far end.
- Targets: anyone in the line of the blast may suffer damage.
- Armour: no effect against Ice Blast.

Resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll damage with the caster's DEX. The basic spell adds 2 weapon damage.

Boost: each additional magic point adds +1 weapon damage.

### Skinny Grenade

Prerequisites: Fireball + Scindere.

Creates a projectile that sticks to the target and explodes after a delay. Blast radius: 2 metres/yards.

Resolve the attack as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll `1D10` to determine the number of rounds before explosion; there is always a delay. When it explodes, roll damage with the caster's DEX. The standard spell adds 1 weapon damage.

Boost: each additional magic point adds +1 weapon damage.

Mastered: the caster chooses the delay, from 1 to 10 rounds.

### Throw Sound

Prerequisites: Shield + Impello.

Throws the caster's voice so it appears to come from elsewhere nearby.

Boost: spend 1 additional magic point to create a different sound, such as a dog barking or a car.

## Fourth Order

### Aqua in Nebula Oborire

Prerequisite: Aqua (mastered).

Raises mist from a suitably large body of water, at minimum a large duck pond or small lake. After 5 minutes, enough mist forms to create a warm bank of vapour 100 metres/yards in diameter. The mist dissipates quickly after 15 minutes.

While inside the mist:

- Stealth rolls gain a bonus die.
- Observation rolls, such as spotting movement through the mist, take a penalty die.

### Coffin Bell, The

Prerequisites: Terra (mastered) + Impello (mastered).

Moves 2 cubic metres/yards of earth or debris.

Boost: each additional magic point moves +1 cubic metre/yard.

### Flying Ice Saw

Prerequisites: Glitter Bomb or Ice Blast.

Creates a 3 metre/yard spinning disk of ice that moves through the air like a circular saw or drill head. Duration: 1 round. It can attack up to 3 separate targets, provided all targets are within 10 metres/yards of each other.

Resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll damage with the caster's DEX. The basic spell adds 2 weapon damage.

Boost: each additional magic point adds +1 weapon damage.

### Natchaduri

Prerequisite: Candano.

Society of the Rose spell, also called Dancing Rope. Creates a short-lived braid of crimson smoke that can bind, slap, or pummel a target up to 15 metres/yards away. It lasts 2 rounds, but the caster can use it for only one action: a combat manoeuvre or a damaging attack.

If used to bind, the target rolls DEX or STR, whichever is higher.

| Target roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hard success or better | Target avoids entanglement or breaks free. |
| Less than Hard success | Target remains bound for 4 rounds. |

If used to harm, resolve as a firearms attack using Magic. Roll damage with the caster's DEX. The spell adds 1 weapon damage.

Climbing with the rope is difficult because it is short-lived and must be attached to something. Depending on the situation, the GM may call for DEX to anchor it, followed by Hard Athletics to climb before it dissipates.

### Telescopium

Prerequisite: Shield (mastered).

Shapes air into two lenses in front of the caster, creating a telescope effect. Poorly shaped lenses can create severe visual distortion, and looking at the sun through the effect can cause blindness.

### Wall Banger, The

Prerequisite: Grasping Hand (mastered).

Rips apart architecture, enough to tear away one face of an average house, including bricks, doors, and windows, and deposit the rubble nearby.

Boost: every 2 additional magic points affects another wall-sized amount of similar scale.

## Fifth Order

### Clausurafrange

Prerequisite: Impello (mastered).

Opens a mundane, non-magical lock while the caster is close to it. With enough time and care it can neatly drill the lock; in a hurry it can force the lock out of its frame.

Boost: spend 1 additional magic point to pick the lock cleanly and leave it undamaged.

### Seducere

Prerequisite: none.

Compels a single target's behaviour. The base fifth-order spell affects one animal, such as a dog. A target rolls POW to resist.

If the target is an animal or non-practitioner:

| POW result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Fumble or failure | Target obeys without question and does not later question why. |
| Regular or Hard success | Target obeys, but later wonders why and rationalises it. |
| Critical success | Target is unaffected, reacts as if the request were mundane, and is unaware of magic. |

If the target is a practitioner or someone aware of glamour:

| POW result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Fumble | Target obeys and is unaware of the spell. |
| Failure | Target begins to act, then recovers self-control. They are aware of the spell. |
| Any success | Target is aware of the spell and is not compelled. |

If an investigator is targeted, they may spend Luck to overcome a failed POW roll. They may also push the resistance roll as a struggle for self-control; a second failure leaves the investigator fully subject to the caster.

Boost: spend 2 additional magic points to affect one human.

Consent note: compulsion spells can remove character agency. Establish table boundaries before using Seducere or Vox Imperante in play.

### Sīphōnem

Prerequisite: none.

Disarms a demon trap by releasing the magical essence powering it. The release may reveal clues about the trap's construction or the creator's signare.

| Trap | Required casting result |
|---|---|
| Standard demon trap | Successful Magic roll. |
| Greater demon trap | Hard success on the Magic roll. |

Failing the casting roll activates a demon trap trigger.

### Vultus Occulto

Prerequisite: Shield (mastered).

Disguises a person by making their face difficult to perceive, as if they have no face. Duration: 10 minutes.

A determined magician can see through the illusion by staring at the caster for a whole round and succeeding on a Magic roll.

Boost: each additional magic point adds 10 minutes of duration.

## Sixth Order

### Heart Stop

Prerequisite: Impello (mastered).

Targets one creature only. After a successful spellcasting roll, resolve opposed POW vs the target's CON. If the target loses, the effect lasts while the caster maintains concentration or 5 minutes, whichever ends first.

| Magic points spent | Total cost | Effect if target loses opposed roll |
|---:|---:|---|
| Base | 6 | 3 damage. Target is immediately Down. The effect presents as temporary paralysis. |
| +1 | 7 | 4 damage in one blow, a Mortal Wound. The effect presents as severe angina or a mild heart attack. |
| +2 | 8 | 5 damage in one attack, a fatal blow. The effect presents as a severe heart attack. |

If an investigator is targeted, they may spend Luck to win the opposed CON vs POW roll, buy off mortal-wound effects, or survive a fatal blow as allowed by the wound rules.

### Vox Imperante

Prerequisite: Seducere.

Issues a command of no more than three words. The caster must see the targets, and the targets must hear the caster. The base spell affects up to four people and/or animals.

Non-named NPCs and animals are affected automatically. Named NPCs and player characters roll POW to resist.

If the target is a named non-practitioner:

| POW result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Fumble or failure | Target obeys without question and does not later question why. |
| Regular or Hard success | Target obeys, but later wonders why and rationalises it. |
| Critical success | Target is unaffected, reacts as if the request were mundane, and is unaware of magic. |

If the target is a named practitioner:

| POW result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Fumble | Target obeys and is unaware of the spell. |
| Failure | Target begins to act, then recovers self-control. They are aware of the spell. |
| Any success | Target is aware of the spell and is not compelled. |

If an investigator is targeted, they may spend Luck to overcome a failed POW roll. They may also push the resistance roll as a struggle for self-control; a second failure leaves the investigator fully subject to the caster.

Boost:

| Additional magic points | Total cost | Maximum targets |
|---:|---:|---:|
| +1 | 7 | 8 |
| +2 | 8 | 16 |
| +3 | 9 | 32 |

Consent note: use the same table-boundary rule as Seducere.
