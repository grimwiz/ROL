# Prompt: Source Inventory

Use with `source/raw/rulebook-relevant.md` as the source body.

Task:

Create an extraction inventory for a compact Rivers of London RPG reference. Do not draft final rules yet.

Requirements:

- Ignore the original rulebook's teaching order.
- Identify all rule subjects, scenario subjects, optional/advanced subjects, and likely duplicates.
- Identify source areas where the markdown appears malformed, out of order, table-damaged, or image-dependent.
- Identify any prerequisites that must appear earlier in the final logical structure than they do in the source.
- Preserve all rule nuance in the inventory: numbers, thresholds, exceptions, prerequisites, and subsystem interactions.
- Mark *The Domestic* and other excluded material as out of scope if present.

Output:

- Proposed logical ordering for the numbered rules files.
- Proposed logical ordering for `scenario/`.
- A missing/uncertain source checklist.
- A table mapping source sections to target files.
