# Character Creation

<!-- Source: character-creation, newtonian-magic-starting-spells, character-sheet-notes. Status: extracted-draft. -->

This file gives the base investigator creation procedure. Optional advanced creation, disadvantages, investigator organisations, and experience packages belong in `12-advanced-options.md`.

## Creation Sequence

Create a standard investigator in this order:

1. Choose an occupation.
2. Assign characteristics.
3. Calculate half values, Luck, MOV, and age.
4. Select advantages.
5. Select starting skills.
6. Add starting magic details if applicable.
7. Define backstory, affluence, and trusted contacts.
8. Record equipment and weapons.

The GM may restrict occupations, supernatural exposure, Folly status, police status, or starting equipment to fit the case file. A Folly-focused group normally includes police officers, civilian consultants, practitioners, or specialists with a reason to work with the Special Assessment Unit.

## Occupation

An occupation defines the investigator's starting skill constraints and a baseline for contacts, equipment, and finances.

- Required occupation skills must be included in the six boosted starting skills unless the occupation gives a specific exception.
- Recommended occupation skills are suggestions, not a closed list.
- Remaining boosted skills may be any common, combat, or expert skills allowed by the normal starting-skill rules.
- An unlisted occupation can be built later with the advanced occupation rules.

## Characteristics

Distribute 280 points among STR, CON, DEX, INT, and POW.

- Each starting characteristic must be between 30 and 80.
- Values are assigned in blocks of 10.
- 50 is average human capability.
- Record each full value and its half value.
- Characteristic rolls use the full value unless a rule calls for a Hard roll.

Advantage prerequisites matter during allocation:

| Characteristic | Advantages enabled at 60+ |
|---|---|
| STR | Damage Bonus, Natural Toughness |
| CON | Natural Toughness |
| DEX | Fast Reactions, Speedy |
| INT | Magical, The Knowledge |
| POW | Magical, Steadfast |

## Luck, MOV, and Age

Starting Luck:

```text
2D10 + 50
```

Starting MOV is 8 unless changed by an advantage or later rule.

The player chooses the investigator's age. The base rules do not alter characteristics, skills, or Luck by age. Some occupations are unavailable or implausible for investigators under 18, including police officer, lawyer, architect, and similar legally regulated professions.

## Advantages

An investigator may select up to two advantages. A major advantage counts as both choices and is the only advantage selected at this step.

If an advantage has a skill prerequisite during character creation, that skill must be one of the investigator's boosted starting skills or otherwise meet the listed value.

| Advantage | Prerequisites | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Connected | None | 1 | Defines access to a powerful person, group, or institution. Significant help can create obligations. The connection may be defined during creation or first used later. Help from the connection normally does not require a Social roll. |
| Damage Bonus | STR 60+ | 1 | Add +1 damage to melee attacks. Does not apply to ranged attacks, firearms, or spells. |
| Fast Reactions | DEX 60+ | 1 | Gain two Fight Back responses per combat round instead of one. These may use melee, firearms, or magic. Opponents gain no benefit from outnumbering this investigator. |
| Magical | INT 60+, POW 60+ | 2 | Grants the aptitude to cast spells and permits selection of the Magic skill. Required for a starting practitioner. |
| Natural Toughness | STR 60+, CON 60+ | 1 | Reduce damage from unarmed attacks and blunt melee weapons by 1. Does not reduce damage from blades, impaling weapons, bullets, or damage-causing spells. |
| Rich | None | 2 | Grants Rich affluence. Required for the Dilettante occupation. |
| Scary | None | 1 | Gain a bonus die on Social rolls made to intimidate. No effect on other Social rolls. |
| Signature Firearm | DEX 60+, Firearms 60+ | 1 | Choose one specific firearm. Gain a bonus die on ranged damage rolls only with that firearm. A replacement requires one month of game time before the advantage applies to it. |
| Signature Weapon | DEX 60+, Fighting 60+ | 1 | Choose one specific melee weapon. Gain a bonus die on damage rolls only with that weapon. A replacement requires one month of game time before the advantage applies to it. |
| Silver-Tongued | None | 1 | Gain a bonus die on Social rolls made to persuade or win someone over through eloquence. No effect on other Social rolls. |
| Speedy | DEX 60+ | 1 | Set MOV to 9. |
| Steadfast | POW 60+ | 1 | Ignore impairment caused by stress, gunfire, grisly sights, or supernatural shocks. Damage, wounds, and Hyperthaumaturgical Degradation can still impair the investigator. |
| The Knowledge | INT 60+, Navigate 60+ | 1 | Choose a city or area. Gain a bonus die on Navigate rolls in that location. |
| Wealthy | None | 1 | Grants Wealthy affluence. |

NPCs may also have advantages. Minor NPCs commonly have none or one, important recurring NPCs may have up to two, and exceptional major NPCs may have three if the profile warrants it.

## Starting Skills

Every standard investigator chooses six skills to boost.

| Skill category | Base value | If boosted at creation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common | 30 | 60 |
| Combat | 30 | 60 |
| Expert | 00 | 60 |

Rules:

- The six boosted skills must include all required skills from the chosen occupation, subject to any occupation-specific exception.
- Recommended occupation skills are optional choices.
- Any remaining boosted skills may be any common, combat, or expert skill.
- Boosted skills are recorded as `60/30`.
- Unboosted common and combat skills remain `30/15`.
- Expert skills only exist on the sheet if selected, granted by a special rule, or acquired later.
- Fighting and Firearms are combat skills, not expert skills.

## Languages

An investigator's own language is free at the higher of INT or 60.

For a bilingual upbringing:

- If equally fluent, the second language is also free at the same value as the first.
- If less fluent, the second language is free at the higher of half INT or 30.

More than two starting languages normally requires selecting Languages as one of the six boosted skills.

## Firearms at Creation

Firearms should fit the setting, occupation, and backstory.

In a UK-based campaign, civilian firearms access is rare and regulated. Plausible starting access includes farmers with licensed shotguns, wealthy sport shooters, some criminals, armed-response police, and service members. Most British police officers do not carry firearms and do not receive firearms training.

## Starting Practitioners

The Magical advantage is required before the Magic skill can be selected. A starting investigator with Magical normally selects Magic as one of the six boosted skills to function as a practitioner.

A starting investigator with Magic gains:

- Magic 60.
- Sense Vestigia 60 for free.
- A signare.
- Two first-order spells: one mastered and one unmastered.
- One second-order spell: unmastered.
- Magic points equal to POW divided by 5, plus 1 for the mastered spell.

Sense Vestigia remains a common skill. Non-practitioners have it at 30 and may choose it as one of the six boosted skills.

Starting spell limits:

- A Newtonian apprentice starts with Werelight as the mastered first-order spell, then chooses the remaining first-order and second-order spells.
- A hedge wizard chooses any two first-order spells and one second-order spell, with one first-order spell mastered.
- Spell prerequisites still apply to all spell choices.
- A mastered spell gives a bonus die to the Magic roll used to cast it.
- The investigator records which spells are mastered. Mastery is permanent.

Signare creation:

- Create a distinctive magical signature for any investigator who has Magical and Magic.
- The signare normally combines sensory elements such as sound, smell, and another sensation.
- If trained by a practitioner with a known signare, include one element from the teacher's signare as an inherited element.
- Full signare detection and use belongs in `09-magic.md`.

In a London-based Folly campaign, practitioners joining the Folly are expected to swear the appropriate practitioner oath. Police practitioners and civilian practitioners use different forms of oath and secrecy agreement.

## Backstory

Backstory may be prepared before play or revealed during play. Required mechanical decisions are limited:

- Name and age.
- Occupation and current employment status.
- Current home or usual base.
- Cultural, family, and social context if relevant to play.
- Whether the investigator has already encountered the supernatural.
- Why the investigator works with the Folly, if applicable.

A prior brush with the supernatural is optional unless the investigator begins play working at the Folly, has Magical, or has Magic. For those investigators, prior supernatural contact is effectively part of the premise.

## Affluence

Affluence replaces detailed personal finance accounting.

- Default affluence is Average.
- Poor may be selected by agreement with the GM.
- Wealthy requires the Wealthy advantage.
- Rich requires the Rich advantage.
- Dilettante requires Rich and always begins Rich.
- Illness, absence from work, recession, inheritance, promotion, or a major change of employment can change affluence during play.
- Routine costs within the living standard normally require no bookkeeping.
- The GM decides whether a purchase exceeds the investigator's living standard or credit.

| Affluence | Standard of living | Accommodation | Travel | Major purchases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poor | Bare minimum shelter and food. | Cheapest rentals or lowest-cost hotels. | Cheapest public transport; any owned vehicle is cheap and unreliable. | None. |
| Average | Stable comfort, regular meals, occasional treats. | Average rented or owned home; moderate hotels. | Standard travel, not first class; likely access to a reliable car. | One per year. |
| Wealthy | Comfortable luxury. | Substantial residence, possible domestic help, possible second home; expensive hotels. | First class; expensive car or equivalent. | One per month. |
| Rich | Extensive luxury. | Plush residence or estate, significant domestic help, second homes; top hotels. | First class or private jet; multiple luxury vehicles, possibly chauffeur-driven. | Few practical limits. |

The Folly can soften the drawbacks of Poor affluence for investigators under its protection by providing food, lodging, and essential work purchases.

## Trusted Contacts

Each investigator should define one or more trusted personal contacts: family, partner, friend, neighbour, colleague, or similar. These people are personally close and normally reliable.

Trusted contacts are not the same as the Connected advantage:

- Trusted contacts are personal relationships.
- Connected represents privileged access to power, resources, or information.
- Connected help may carry explicit obligations.

If using the character sheet contact boxes, mark a good relationship as Secure and a damaged relationship as Sour.

## Starting Equipment

An investigator begins with equipment reasonable for the occupation, current duty status, affluence, and backstory.

- Occupational tools and routine possessions do not need itemised purchase.
- Additional items may be bought at creation if available and within the investigator's living standard.
- The GM has final approval over unusual, restricted, expensive, or story-significant items.
- Record only notable, useful, restricted, or unusual equipment.

Folly credentials:

- Police officers seconded to the Folly keep their warrant card or equivalent police credentials and police powers.
- Civilian consultants who swear the required oath receive a Folly consultant's card and sign a non-disclosure agreement.
- A consultant's card proves official Folly status but does not grant full police powers.
- A consultant's card does not grant the Social bonus that uniformed or credentialed police officers receive with law-abiding citizens.

## Weapons

Weapon damage starts from base weapon damage. After a successful attack, use the combat damage roll rules to determine additional damage.

General weapon rules:

- Melee weapons use Fighting.
- Thrown melee weapons may use Athletics where listed.
- Bows, crossbows, firearms, and similar ranged weapons use Firearms.
- Most melee weapons and non-firearm projectile weapons have base damage 1.
- Regular handguns have base damage 1.
- Heavy handguns, rifles, shotguns, submachine guns, and machine guns have base damage 2.
- Unarmed attacks have base damage 0.

If Signature Weapon or Signature Firearm was selected, name the exact weapon now.

### Melee Weapons

| Weapon | Skill | Base damage | Base range |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Axe | Fighting or Athletics | 1 | Touch or STR/5 |
| Brass knuckles | Fighting | 1 | Touch |
| Chainsaw | Fighting | 2 | Touch |
| Cricket/baseball bat | Fighting | 1 | Touch |
| Improvised object | Fighting or Athletics | 1 | Touch or STR/5 |
| Knife | Fighting or Athletics | 1 | Touch or STR/5 |
| PAVA spray or mace | Fighting | Special | Up to 3 metres/yards |
| Police baton | Fighting | 1 | Touch |
| Spear | Fighting or Athletics | 1 | Touch or STR/5 |
| Unarmed | Fighting | 0 | Touch |

Thrown entries use the Athletics throwing rules where relevant.

PAVA spray or mace: on a successful attack, the target makes a Hard CON roll. On a failure, the target is blinded and in pain for `1D6` rounds. While blinded, every action has a penalty die. PAVA spray and mace do not gain a point-blank range bonus die.

### Firearms and Ranged Weapons

| Weapon | Skill | Base damage | Base range |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Bow | Firearms | 1 | 30 metres/yards |
| Crossbow | Firearms | 1 | 50 metres/yards |
| Heavy handgun | Firearms | 2 | 15 metres/yards |
| Machine gun | Firearms | 2 | 100 metres/yards |
| Regular handgun | Firearms | 1 | 15 metres/yards |
| Rifle | Firearms | 2 | 100 metres/yards |
| Shotgun | Firearms | 2 | 50 metres/yards |
| Stun gun | Firearms | Special | Up to 3 metres/yards |
| Submachine gun | Firearms | 2 | 15 metres/yards |

Machine guns, assault rifles, and submachine guns can use automatic fire rules where those rules are in play.

Stun gun: on a successful attack, the target makes a Hard CON roll. On a failure, the target is stunned for `1D6` rounds and every action has a penalty die. Stun guns do not gain a point-blank range bonus die.
