# Logical Outline

Last updated: 2026-05-23.

## Cross-Reference Rules

- Do not duplicate full procedures across files. Link to the canonical rule file.
- Keep combat-magic references in both `06-combat` and `09-magic`, but place the full spellcasting procedure in `09-magic`.
- Keep damage condition definitions in `07-damage-and-healing`; other files may mention them by name.
- Keep base player character creation options in `03-character-creation`. Keep optional/advanced creation source in `../rules-advanced-source/12-advanced-options.md` until a reviewed advanced corpus exists; scenario NPC summaries should not redefine PC creation.

## Intentionally Omitted by Default

- *The Domestic* solo adventure.
- Full Chapter 8 adventure text.
- Pregenerated investigator backstories and sheet blocks.
- Fiction, forewords, author commentary, contributor biographies, bibliography, index, marketing copy.
- Map and handout images, unless later needed as private scenario assets.
