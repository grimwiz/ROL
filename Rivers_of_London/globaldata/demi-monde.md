## What "the demi-monde" means

The **demi-monde** is the half-hidden world of the supernatural that runs
alongside ordinary London — the beings, communities, and powers that most
people never knowingly meet. The Folly's job is partly to keep that world and
the mundane one from colliding messily, and to uphold the old agreements that
keep an uneasy peace.

Demi-monde beings share some common traits in play: many have **Luck equal to
their POW that refreshes once a day**, a **thematic power** (an affinity with
some element or concept), and — for the more person-like ones — the **glamour**,
the knack of swaying a human mind. Many are also vulnerable to **cold iron**.

## The fae

"Fae" is a broad label for a class of demi-monde being rather than a single
species. They are not human, though some pass for it, and they run from the
small and local to the genuinely powerful.

- **High Fae** — tall, striking, unsettlingly elegant beings with strong
  affinities; powerful and not to be trifled with. (Foxglove, the Folly's
  resident artist, is High Fae.)
- **Lower fae and others** — a wide range of lesser fae, hidden folk, and
  creatures of folklore, each tied to a place, an element, or a theme.

A practical note: fae and humans reckon obligation, hospitality, and bargains
very seriously — accepting a gift or a favour can carry real weight.

## The river folk (genii locorum)

The river-spirits are demi-monde powers in their own right — Father Thames and
Mama Thames and their tributary families. They get their own reference:
`rivers-and-genii-locorum.md`.

## The Quiet People

The **Quiet People** are a community that lives underground beneath London —
"born different", tied to the earth and the buried infrastructure of the city,
and connected in places to the Underground. They are their own kind of people,
not ghosts and not quite like the fae, with their own customs and a strong
preference for being left alone.

## Ghosts

Ghosts are imprints of personality left on a place after death, powered by the
magic stored in stone and old buildings. They range from faint **loopers** and
limited **simulacra** up to **entities** that seem to think and respond, with
**revenants** and other nastier things at the edges. Fuller notes — types, the
intensity scale, what gets mistaken for a ghost — are in `magic-overview.md`.

## Other powers

The world also holds rarer beings — unicorns, the genii locorum of places
outside London, beings of older traditions, and creatures that don't fit a neat
box. The constant is that each should be read on its own terms: powers to be
dealt with through knowledge, courtesy, and caution, not simply fought.
